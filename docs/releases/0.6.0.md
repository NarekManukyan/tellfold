# Tellfold 0.6.0

- **A crowd built from your own audience.** The New Simulation sheet has a new step, "Who is the audience?". Add customer reviews, survey answers, support emails, social comments or meeting notes as .txt, .md, .csv, .tsv or .eml files, and the people in the run are written from what your audience actually said. Each person stands for a group of similar comments, and the camps follow how your material splits. For a spreadsheet, you pick the column to read. The cast editor and the person inspector show the quotes each person was written from, and the report says what the crowd was based on.
- **Cleaned on your Mac first.** Before anything goes to the model, Tellfold removes common names, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, links and order or account numbers, plus any words you add under "Other names to hide". The preview shows every item exactly as it will be sent. A name the rules don't know can slip through, so give the preview a look. The run keeps only the cleaned text: your files and their names are never stored. If you export a `.tellfold` bundle of such a run, it carries the cleaned quotes, and the export panel tells you so.
- **From the command line and MCP too.** `tellfold run --material reviews:reviews.txt` builds a grounded run, and `tellfold material` previews what would be sent. In MCP, `start_simulation` and `compare_drafts` take `material` and `hide`, and a new `preview_material` tool shows the cleaned items first.
- **Limits.** A run reads up to 20 files and keeps at most 300 items, picked evenly when there are more. Reading the material adds a few model calls, and the cost line on the sheet says how many.
