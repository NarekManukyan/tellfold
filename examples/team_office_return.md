# Coming back together: our new working week from 1 June

*Sent by email to all staff of Halden Freight Software, a 140-person logistics software company, by its chief executive. Fictional document written as an example seed for Tellfold.*

Hi everyone,

Since 2021 most of us have worked from home most of the time, and the work has been good. We shipped the new route planner, we kept every big customer and we hired 50 people, half of whom have never met the other half.

That last part is the problem. Onboarding now takes our new engineers about five months, against two and a half before 2020. Support and engineering mostly talk through tickets. In the staff survey in March, 41% said they did not feel part of a team, and only 23% had met their manager's manager in person.

So, from **Monday 1 June**, our working week changes:

- **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are office days** for everyone who lives within 90 minutes of the Ostbrück office.
- Monday and Friday stay home days. No meetings before 10 am on any day.
- The **12 people hired as fully remote** stay fully remote. Their teams will meet in person one week each quarter, and we pay for the travel.
- Product, engineering and support **move into mixed teams** of 8 to 10, each owning one part of the product, with one shared desk area per team.
- Two managers' roles change: support and engineering now report to the same head of product, Mareike Lindqvist.

What we are adding:

- A €90 monthly travel allowance for office days, paid with salary.
- Lunch in the office on all three days.
- The second floor becomes quiet space: no calls, no meetings.

What we are not doing:

- Nobody will be tracked by badge swipes or laptop activity.
- Nobody's pay or job depends on the office move.
- If the office days make your caring duties or health harder, talk to People Team before 15 May. We will agree an exception, in writing, with no questions about the details.

I know some of you moved further away during the last five years because you could. If that is you, please talk to us before you decide anything. We would rather find a way than lose you.

We will look at the survey numbers again in December and change this if it is not working. There is an open Q&A on Thursday 7 May at 4 pm, in the office and on video.

Jonas Reuter
Chief executive, Halden Freight Software
