# Letter to parents: phones at Westbrook Secondary from September

*Sent by email and printed in the school newsletter of Westbrook Secondary School. Fictional document written as an example seed for Tellfold.*

Dear parents and carers,

After a year of discussion with staff, students and the parents' council, Westbrook will become a **phone-free school day** from the start of the autumn term on 3 September.

## What will happen

- Students may still bring a phone to school.
- On arrival, phones are switched off and locked in a personal pouch. The pouch stays with the student, but it can only be opened at the unlocking stations by the main gate at the end of the day.
- This applies during lessons, breaks and lunch, for all year groups (ages 11 to 18).
- Sixth-form students (ages 16 to 18) may unlock their phones in the sixth-form common room during free periods.
- Pouches cost the school €18 each. We will not ask families to pay for them.

## Why we are doing this

In a survey last spring, 64% of our students said they feel pressure to reply to messages during the school day, and 41% said they had seen something upsetting on a phone at school in the last month. Teachers report that lessons regularly stop because of phones. Three schools nearby made the same change last year and report calmer breaks and fewer incidents of online bullying during school hours.

## Contacting your child

If you need to reach your child during the day, call the school office on 01 555 0192 and we will pass on the message straight away. Students with medical needs that rely on a phone (for example glucose monitoring) will be exempt; please contact Ms Duarte, our pastoral lead, before 1 August.

## What happens if a student breaks the rule

The first time, the phone is kept at the office until the end of the day. After the third time in a term, a parent or carer must collect it in person.

## Have your say

We know some of you have concerns, especially about travel to and from school, and about emergencies. Please join us at the information evening on **Tuesday 17 June at 6:30 pm** in the main hall, or reply to this email.

Yours sincerely,
Dr Amara Lewis, Headteacher
