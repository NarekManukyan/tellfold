# Outcome: Claude Code weekly limits

## Timeline

- 2026-08-29 — Developer Manton Reece writes that the announcement was made "in the most convoluted way possible" and is "actually bad news" once the 17% figure is understood ([manton.org, 29 Aug 2026](https://www.manton.org/2026/08/29/anthropic-announced-a-limits-change.html)).
- 2026-08-29 — BleepingComputer calls the framing "clever" and the change a downgrade; Anthropic deletes the original thread and posts a clarification confirming "a 17% reduction" compared with today. Some commenters push back that the boost was always temporary ([BleepingComputer, 29 Aug 2026](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-is-cutting-claude-codes-current-weekly-limits-by-17-percent/)).
- 2026-08-29 — Notebookcheck reports users attached a Community Note to the main post warning of the reduction, because the admission was buried in a reply ([Notebookcheck, 29 Aug 2026](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Anthropic-announces-a-25-increase-to-Claude-Code-limits-but-there-s-a-17-catch.1382735.0.html)).
- 2026-08-31 — Implicator quotes Reddit users: one says Anthropic "burn[ed] all their goodwill" with vague plan labels, another says they check usage every 30 minutes but will keep Claude as their main tool ([Implicator, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-claude-code-weekly-limits-september-14/)).
- 2026-09-14 — New limits scheduled to take effect; no change to the plan was reported.

## Overall reaction

Negative. The objection was less the new limit itself than the way it was presented: a cut in current capacity announced as a 25% increase. Users added a Community Note and tech outlets headlined the 17% reduction. A minority said the boost was always temporary, and some heavy users said they would stay despite rationing.

## What people objected to

- Misleading framing: a 17% cut in current limits presented as a 25% increase.
- Less weekly capacity for paying subscribers, forcing heavy users to ration.
- Opaque limits and plan labels that do not say how much usage subscribers get.

## Who pushed back

- Paying Claude Code subscribers, especially heavy Max-plan developers.
- Developer bloggers and tech press (BleepingComputer, Notebookcheck).

## Did they back down?

No. Anthropic clarified the 17% figure but kept the 14 September change; no reversal was reported in the sources checked (latest checked: 31 Aug 2026, plus a Notebookcheck update dated 5 Oct 2026 that still describes the change).
