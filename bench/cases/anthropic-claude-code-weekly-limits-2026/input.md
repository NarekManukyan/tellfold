# Anthropic sets new permanent Claude Code weekly limits as its temporary 50% boost ends

## Who the body is

Anthropic makes the Claude AI models and sells consumer and business subscriptions (Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise). Claude Code, its coding agent, is popular with software developers, and its usage on subscription plans is capped by a five-hour session limit and a weekly limit. On 13 May 2026 Anthropic gave subscribers a temporary 50% boost to weekly Claude Code limits; it was extended several times over the summer. Rival OpenAI had recently tightened limits on its own coding tools.

## What was announced

On Saturday 29 August 2026 Anthropic announced on X, through its developer account, that:

- From 14 September 2026 it is "permanently raising standard weekly limits in Claude Code by 25%" for Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans.
- The temporary 50% boost stays in place until then and ends when the new standard takes effect.
- In practice, if the pre-May allowance is 100 units, users currently have 150 and will have 125 from 14 September: 25% above the old standard, about 17% below what they have today.
- Five-hour session limits are unchanged. Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats are not affected.
- Anthropic said it is working on changes to give users more visibility and control over their usage, without giving details or dates, and noted that weekly allowances do not map to a fixed number of prompts because usage depends on conversation length, model, tools and effort level.

## Stated reasons

- Making a higher baseline permanent rather than relying on temporary promotions, framed around long-term sustainability and platform stability.

## How it was communicated

A thread on X from Anthropic's ClaudeDevs account, with the headline post focused on the 25% permanent increase and the comparison with current limits given in a reply.
