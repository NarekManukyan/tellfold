# Apple Music unveils a new brand identity, moving from red to black and white

*Benchmark seed: a neutral summary of the announcement as it stood on 2026-10-21, written for the Tellfold accuracy test. Not the original text.*

## Who is announcing

Apple Music is Apple's music streaming service, launched in 2015 and available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Android, Windows and the web. Its app icon, white musical notes on a red-to-pink gradient (the "beamed eighth note"), has been in use for about 13 years, going back to the iTunes era. Red and pink accents have been part of the app's interface throughout. In November 2025 Apple refreshed the Apple TV brand with a similar behind-the-scenes "making of" approach. Since 2025 Apple's software has used the translucent "Liquid Glass" design language.

## What was announced

On Wednesday 21 October 2026 Apple formally unveiled a new identity for Apple Music under the idea that it is "made by sound".

- **Colour:** The brand moves from red to a black-and-white system. The app icon becomes black notes on white, or white on black in Dark Mode. Red accents in the app and widgets are replaced; the in-app tint becomes blue.
- **Wordmark:** The Apple logo plus "music" in bold, all-lowercase type. Reports describe it as a heavier, rounder, more tightly spaced face; some say it is a customised version of Apple's SF Pro. This wordmark was first shown in September 2026 at the opening of Apple Music Hall, a concert venue in London.
- **How it was made:** Apple's agency TBWA\Media Arts Lab worked with the producer Fred again.. and the audio design firm NEST Acoustics. Individual notes were played through a transducer into a bath of mineral oil, and the ripples were filmed in camera. Those forms became the reworked note icon and an animated logo. A short composition by Fred again.. and NEST Acoustics becomes Apple Music's sonic signature.
- **Rollout:** The new identity appears on Apple Music's website and original content from this week. The app icon and interface changes arrive with iOS 27.2 and macOS 27.2, expected the following week. The new icon was already visible in the iOS 27.2 developer beta released on 13 October.
- **What does not change:** The update is visual only. There are no new plans, features or price changes.

**Stated rationale:** Apple presents the identity as coming from music itself, built through craft and collaboration with artists rather than designed on screen, and giving Apple Music a personality of its own. Apple published a short "Made by Sound" behind-the-scenes video. The tagline is "Just music, made visible."

## How it was communicated

A behind-the-scenes video and brand launch on 21 October 2026, with press coverage in design and music outlets, ahead of the iOS 27.2 software update.
