# Outcome: Apple Music black-and-white rebrand (officially unveiled 2026-10-21)

## Timeline

- 2026-10-13 — iOS 27.2 beta 4 shows the new black-and-white icon before the official reveal. Cult of Mac reports an immediate, overwhelmingly negative reaction: users call it generic and boring, liken it to a piano-lesson app, and some demand Apple revert it ([Cult of Mac](https://cultofmac.com/news/apple-music-app-icon-change-ios-27-beta)).
- 2026-10-14 — Engadget: "People are hating on Apple Music's redesigned black-and-white logo". Most found it dull and unnecessary, one commenter called it "the worst logo change of all time", and a minority liked it ([Engadget](https://engadget.com/2286289/people-are-hating-on-apple-music-redesigned-black-and-white-logo/)).
- 2026-10-20/21 — Apple officially reveals the "made by sound" identity. Reports date it 20 or 21 October; The Fader says Wednesday 21 October. Coverage notes users remain largely negative, calling the icon dull and a downgrade, while design enthusiasts praise the practical-effects process ([Gagadget](https://gagadget.com/en/731157-apple-musics-red-icon-goes-black-and-white-and-users-arent-happy-amp/); [The Fader](https://www.thefader.com/2026/10/21/fred-again-helped-apple-music-build-its-new-look-out-of-sound-waves)).
- 2026-10-21 — Design press covers the process positively, for example Wallpaper's enthusiastic feature ([Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/art/music/apple-musics-new-visual-identity-turns-sound-into-liquid-with-help-from-fred-again)).
- 2026-10-23 — Appleosophy describes reactions as mixed, with users missing the red, and says Apple appears committed to the new direction ([Appleosophy](https://appleosophy.com/2026/10/23/from-red-note-to-black-and-white-apple-musics-new-identity/)).
- ~2026-11-06 — Apple extends the look to Apple Music Classical (version 4.1, black-and-white treble clef). Engadget reports less backlash for this one ([Gagadget](https://gagadget.com/en/733294-apple-music-classical-goes-black-and-white-too/)).

## Overall reaction

Negative among everyday users, who mainly felt the loss of the familiar red: dull, unnecessary, a downgrade. Design and music press were warmer, especially about the sound-driven process. Some users liked the cleaner look. Much of the user backlash began a week before the official reveal, when the icon leaked through the developer beta.

## What people objected to

- The new icon is dull, boring and generic ([Engadget](https://engadget.com/2286289/people-are-hating-on-apple-music-redesigned-black-and-white-logo/); [Cult of Mac](https://cultofmac.com/news/apple-music-app-icon-change-ios-27-beta)).
- Dropping the red removes the app's recognisable identity and charm ([Appleosophy](https://appleosophy.com/2026/10/23/from-red-note-to-black-and-white-apple-musics-new-identity/); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2286289/people-are-hating-on-apple-music-redesigned-black-and-white-logo/)).
- The change is unnecessary, with no new features to justify it ([Gagadget](https://gagadget.com/en/731157-apple-musics-red-icon-goes-black-and-white-and-users-arent-happy-amp/)).
- It looks like a different kind of app (a piano-lesson app) and is harder to spot on the home screen ([Cult of Mac](https://cultofmac.com/news/apple-music-app-icon-change-ios-27-beta)).

## Who pushed back

Long-time Apple Music and iPhone users on X and in comment sections, and Apple-focused blogs. Design enthusiasts and design press were more positive.

## Did they back down?

No. The identity rolled out as planned, and Apple extended the same black-and-white treatment to Apple Music Classical in early November.
