# Outcome: Nintendo Switch 2 price rise (announced 2026-05-08) — CONTROL CASE

## Timeline

- 2026-05-08 — Nintendo announces the rise with its earnings: US $499.99 from 1 September, Japan ¥59,980 from 25 May, Switch Online prices rising in Japan ([Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/05/08/the-switch-2-is-getting-a-price-increase); [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/nintendo-switch-2-price-increased-to-nearly-700-in-some-regions/)).
- 2026-05-08 — VICE calls the near-C$700 Canadian price "staggering" next to the PS5, and notes reports that investors had pushed Nintendo to raise prices ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/nintendo-switch-2-price-increased-to-nearly-700-in-some-regions/)).
- 2026-05-13 — Market Realist rounds up unhappy fans on X: people who had waited for a price cut, people who said it was already overpriced, and people who said a $500 handheld is no longer the cheap option. One fan said a bundle with a free game at $499 would be a good deal. Analyst Serkan Toto says Nintendo urgently needs blockbuster games ([Market Realist](https://marketrealist.com/consumer/nintendo-switch-2-price-increase/)).
- 2026-08-28 — The rise is still scheduled for 1 September. Circana data shows US console unit sales fell sharply in July as average console prices rose ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Switch-2-price-hike-to-take-effect-next-week-even-as-console-sales-take-a-hit-in-July.1381459.0.html)).
- 2026-09-01 — Scheduled effective date for the new US price. Coverage a few days earlier still described it as going ahead unchanged, and no reversal was found ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Switch-2-price-hike-to-take-effect-next-week-even-as-console-sales-take-a-hit-in-July.1381459.0.html)).

## Overall reaction

Negative among fans and in games media, but resigned rather than angry. Many saw it as part of an industry-wide trend, since Sony and Microsoft had raised prices more. There was no organised campaign. Some commentators said the rise was smaller than rivals' increases and possibly inevitable given component costs.

## What people objected to

- The console was already too expensive, and $500 is a lot for a Nintendo handheld ([Market Realist](https://marketrealist.com/consumer/nintendo-switch-2-price-increase/)).
- Fans waiting for a price cut got a rise instead and said they would buy nothing ([Market Realist](https://marketrealist.com/consumer/nintendo-switch-2-price-increase/)).
- Regional prices, especially near C$700 in Canada, look poor value next to a more powerful PS5 ([VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/nintendo-switch-2-price-increased-to-nearly-700-in-some-regions/)).
- Higher prices on a console whose sales Nintendo already expects to fall make the strategy look questionable ([Market Realist](https://marketrealist.com/consumer/nintendo-switch-2-price-increase/)).

## Who pushed back

Prospective buyers waiting for a discount, existing fans on social media, Canadian and other non-US buyers, and games journalists. Industry analysts were more neutral.

## Did they back down?

No. The increase was kept and was still going ahead days before its 1 September 2026 effective date; no reversal or delay was reported. Nintendo gave buyers about four months' notice.
