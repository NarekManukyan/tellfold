# Nintendo raises the recommended price of Switch 2

*Benchmark seed: a neutral summary of the announcement as it stood on 2026-05-08, written for the Tellfold accuracy test. Not the original text. CONTROL CASE: announced before the model's June 2026 training cutoff.*

## Who is announcing

Nintendo makes the Switch family of games consoles and its own games (Mario, Zelda, Pokémon with partners and others). The Switch 2 launched worldwide on 5 June 2025 at $449.99 in the US, €469.99 in Europe, C$629.99 in Canada and ¥49,980 in Japan. It has sold close to 20 million units. Over the past year Sony and Microsoft have raised PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series console prices several times, and the original Switch also got more expensive in the US. Memory chips and other components have become more expensive and harder to get across the electronics industry.

## What was announced

On 8 May 2026, alongside its annual financial results, Nintendo announced revised recommended prices.

**United States, Canada and Europe, from 1 September 2026** (standalone Switch 2 console only):

- US: $449.99 to **$499.99**
- Canada: C$629.99 to **C$679.99**
- Europe: €469.99 to **€499.99**

**Japan, from 25 May 2026:**

- Switch 2: ¥49,980 to **¥59,980** (the multi-language model sold on the My Nintendo Store keeps its current price)
- Original Switch models also rise: Switch OLED to ¥47,980, Switch to ¥43,980, Switch Lite to ¥29,980
- Nintendo Switch Online subscription prices in Japan rise from 1 July

Nintendo said it may revise prices in some other regions, without details. The new US price matches the price of the Mario Kart World bundle, which Nintendo stopped producing and now sells in limited quantities. North American and European buyers have about four months to buy at the current price.

**Stated reason:** Changes in market conditions, which Nintendo expects to last over the medium to long term. Nintendo did not name specific costs. At the same results, Nintendo forecast Switch 2 unit sales for the coming fiscal year at about 16.5 million, down from about 19.9 million.

## How it was communicated

Announced with Nintendo's full-year earnings on 8 May 2026 through official news releases and regional Nintendo websites, and reported by games and business media the same day.
