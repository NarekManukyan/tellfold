# Costco brings its food court churro back to US warehouses for a limited time

## Who the body is

Costco is a US membership warehouse chain whose low-priced food courts are a familiar part of a warehouse visit. Its original straight churro, sold from the 1990s at 99 cents, was replaced around 2021 by a "Twisted Churro", which was itself taken off US food court menus in early 2024. For about two years since then, members have asked online for it to come back. Earlier in 2026 Costco put mini churro bites on a $2.99 caramel sundae, and this year it also added chicken tenders and a hot dog and bottled water combo to the food court.

## What was announced

In mid-September 2026, after a short Instagram video in which two employees discussed a returning food court item whose name was bleeped out, Costco revealed the item and confirmed that:

- The Twisted Churro is returning to food courts at all US warehouses.
- It will be sold for a limited time; no end date was given.
- The expected price is $1.49, the same as when it was dropped.
- Launch dates vary by warehouse, rolling out over several weeks.

The returning item is the twisted version, not the original straight churro. No other discontinued food court items were announced alongside it.

## Stated reasons

Costco did not give a detailed reason beyond bringing back an item members had asked for. It did not say how long the churro would stay.

## How it was communicated

A teaser video on Costco's Instagram account, followed by the reveal of the item and its price on social media and in statements reported by the press.
