# Outcome: Costco churro return

## Timeline

- 2026-09-17 — After Costco's teaser of a mystery returning item, the Twisted Churro is revealed. Tasting Table reports that many fans are not excited: Instagram and Reddit users say the twisted version is worse and pricier than the old straight churro, call it "always disappointing", and say they would rather have the combo pizza (one such comment drew over 300 likes) or the Polish dog. Some social media users were excited ([Tasting Table, 17 Sep 2026](https://www.tastingtable.com/2261748/costco-food-court-churros-return-fans-unimpressed/)).
- 2026-09-19 — Fox News Digital reports excited Instagram comments ("Dreams do come true", "FINALLY", a parent describing their child screaming) alongside complaints that it is only for a limited time, that fans wanted the straight churro and not the "twisted imposters", and calls for the combo pizza next ([Fox News, 19 Sep 2026](https://www.foxnews.com/food-drink/costco-brings-back-fan-favorite-food-court-treat-years-off-menu.print)).
- 2026-09-24 — On Costco's fourth-quarter earnings call, CFO Gary Millerchip confirms the "rumors are true" and that the churro returns to all US locations for a limited time, after speculation that had been "blowing up" Costco's social feeds ([CFO Dive, 25 Sep 2026](https://www.cfodive.com/news/costco-cfo-doubles-down-tariff-strategy-winks-churro-comeback/831362/)).
- 2026-09-30 — Local coverage confirms the $1.49 price at every US warehouse and that it is unknown how long it will stay ([MyNorthwest, 30 Sep 2026](https://mynorthwest.com/local/costco-churro-food-court/4281558)).

## Overall reaction

Mixed. Excitement from fans who had asked for the churro for two years sat alongside sizeable disappointment that it is the twisted rather than the original straight churro, that it is only temporary, and that other missed items (combo pizza, Polish dog) were not the ones returning. The tone was light; nobody organised against it.

## What people objected to

- It is the twisted churro, seen as worse than the pre-COVID straight churro.
- It is only for a limited time.
- Members would rather see the combo pizza or the Polish hot dog return.
- Quality complaints about the twisted version (dry, doughy, sitting out too long).
- The mystery teaser raised expectations the churro did not meet.

## Who pushed back

- Long-time food court fans who preferred the original straight churro.
- Fans campaigning for the combo pizza and Polish hot dog.

Supporters included fans and families who had asked for the churro to return.

## Did they back down?

No change. Costco's CFO confirmed the limited-time return on 24 September and it rolled out at the stated price.
