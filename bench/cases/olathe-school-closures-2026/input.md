# Olathe Public Schools recommends closing six schools

*Benchmark seed: a neutral summary of the announcement as it stood on 3 September 2026, written for the Tellfold accuracy test. Not the original text.*

## Who the district is

Olathe Public Schools is a large suburban K-12 district in Johnson County, Kansas, in the Kansas City metro area. It runs 33 elementary schools plus middle and high schools and enrols about 28,000 students. Enrolment peaked at roughly 30,300 in 2019-20 and has fallen since. Earlier in 2026 the district had already agreed to merge two pairs of elementary schools.

In February 2026 the district started a three-phase review of its buildings. A task force of more than 100 community members recommended how many schools should close, and over the summer a 20-person steering committee picked the specific buildings using around 40 data points per school (enrolment, building age and condition, maintenance costs, transport and geography).

## What was announced

At the Board of Education meeting on Thursday 3 September 2026, district staff recommended closing six schools at the end of the 2026-27 school year:

- Five elementary schools: Black Bob, Heritage, Scarborough, Tomahawk and Walnut Grove.
- One middle school: Indian Trail.

Key points from the presentation:

- Estimated savings of at least $12 million a year.
- The number of elementary schools would fall from 33 to 28. Each affected school has a few hundred students.
- Two of the closed buildings would temporarily house students from other consolidating schools; the rest would probably be sold.
- Specialised programmes, including Scarborough's deaf and hard-of-hearing programme, would be moved, not cut.
- The deputy superintendent for operations said no employees would lose their jobs in the transition.

## Stated reasons

- Falling enrolment, with further decline projected over the next 15 years.
- Budget pressure, including rising costs and state funding for special education that the district says falls short.
- The district says last year's bond vote is separate from the closure process.

## Next steps as announced

- One public hearing on 22 September 2026 at Olathe South High School.
- Board vote expected on 1 October 2026.
- Boundary committee meetings October to December, with new attendance boundaries to be approved in January 2027, ahead of the 2027-28 school year.
- The district says transition support (school visits, wellbeing teams) will follow the vote.
