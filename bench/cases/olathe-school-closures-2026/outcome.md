# Outcome: Olathe Public Schools six-school closure plan

## Timeline

- 2026-09-03 — District staff recommend closing Black Bob, Heritage, Scarborough, Tomahawk and Walnut Grove elementaries and Indian Trail Middle at the end of 2026-27, citing falling enrolment and at least $12M a year in savings ([KSHB: Olathe School District recommends closing 6 schools](https://www.kshb.com/news/local-news/shifting-education/olathe-school-district-recommends-closing-6-schools-at-thursday-school-board-meeting)).
- 2026-09-04 — Parents tell local TV they were blindsided; one school's PTO president reports more than 80 texts from families the night of the news. Parents say they will attend the hearing and start a petition ([KCTV5, 4 Sep 2026](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/09/04/olathe-district-recommends-closing-six-schools-citing-declining-enrollment/)).
- 2026-09-22 — The only public hearing before the vote draws hundreds of parents, students and staff to Olathe South High. Speakers question the data, the choice of larger schools, transport, the effect on Black Bob's language immersion programme and on students with IEPs, and ask for the vote to be delayed until a school-by-school impact analysis is published ([KCTV5, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/09/23/olathe-school-district-community-questions-speaks-out-against-potential-school-closures/); [Spectrum News, 23 Sep 2026](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/mo/kansas-city/news/2026/09/23/olathe-community-shares-concerns-over-school-closures)).
- 2026-10-01 — Board votes 7-0 to close all six schools at the end of 2026-27, with little discussion. About 50 people attend; five speak against. Members describe a hard decision and point to their fiduciary duty ([KCUR, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.kcur.org/education/2026-10-02/olathe-public-schools-closures)).
- 2026-10-11 — Parent group Olathe Families United is gathering signatures (target 6,100 valid) to force an advisory review by the Kansas State Board of Education under K.S.A. 72-1431 ([KCTV5, 11 Oct 2026](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/10/12/olathe-parents-gather-signatures-seeking-state-review-school-closures/)).
- 2026-11-02 — The petition passes 6,322 signatures against a 6,115 threshold; organisers raise their goal to 8,200 to allow for invalid entries, with materials due 10 November. They say the aim is to slow the process, not stop it permanently ([KCTV5, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/11/02/olathe-school-closure-petition-surpasses-6300-signatures-group-raises-goal-8200/)).

## Overall reaction

Negative among affected families. Parents of the six schools organised quickly, filled the one public hearing and then ran a petition drive. The complaints were mostly about process and evidence rather than a flat refusal to close any school: families said they did not have enough data, had too little time and had no say in which schools were picked. No reported group spoke in favour of the specific list.

## What people objected to

- Parents felt blindsided and said the district's communication before the announcement fell short.
- They doubted the data and asked why schools with larger enrolments were chosen.
- They wanted boundary scenarios, transport plans and a school-by-school impact analysis before any vote, and asked for a delay.
- Black Bob's dual-language immersion programme: students cannot normally join after kindergarten, so moving them breaks up cohorts.
- Effects on students with special needs (several hundred students with IEPs across the schools) and on transport and logistics.
- One public hearing was seen as too little consultation.

## Who pushed back

- Parents at the six schools, including PTO leaders at Tomahawk and Walnut Grove.
- A new parent group, Olathe Families United (also campaigning as "6,100 Voices"), which ran the state-review petition.
- Some students and staff spoke at the hearing.

## Did they back down?

No. The board approved the full recommendation 7-0 on 1 October, unchanged, and moved on to boundary planning. As of early November the petition for a state review had passed its signature threshold, but any state review is advisory only and cannot force a reversal. Its outcome was still pending at the time of writing.
