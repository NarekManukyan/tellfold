# Outcome: Jetstar overhead locker fee

## Timeline

- 2026-08-05 — Jetstar announces the Priority Carry-on fee for overhead lockers from February 2027. The CEO of AirlineRatings.com tells AP the policy will ruffle feathers because Australians are used to full-service options ([AP via WTOP, 5 Aug 2026](https://wtop.com/europe/2026/08/australian-airline-jetstar-will-charge-passengers-to-use-the-overhead-lockers-for-their-bags/); [The Nightly, 5 Aug 2026](https://thenightly.com.au/business/jetstar-scraps-7kg-carry-on-baggage-limit-in-major-overhaul-for-australia-flights-from-february-2027-c-22677692)).
- 2026-08-05 — Passengers interviewed at Melbourne Airport are mostly shocked: they call it "absurd" and "disgusting", say it makes low-cost flying harder, and a young family says they need the locker space. One woman calls it a great idea because of oversized bags. A former flight attendant says it hurts customers and asks where the transparency is ([The Nightly, 5 Aug 2026](https://thenightly.com.au/australia/passengers-and-former-flight-attendant-weigh-in-on-jetstar-carry-on-cost-changes-c-22678859)).
- 2026-08-06 — Opposition senator Bridget McKenzie calls the fee a "cash grab"; Transport Minister Catherine King says airlines must disclose extra fees at booking and that Jetstar will have to show passengers it keeps fares affordable. Consumer advocates are reported as criticising add-on fees generally ([FOX 7 Austin / FOX Business, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/jetstar-overhead-carry-on-bag-fee-2027)).
- 2026-08-06 — AirlineRatings publishes a counter-view: dropping the 7 kg limit and gate weighing is a real improvement and the anger is aimed at the wrong thing (base fares) ([AirlineRatings, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.airlineratings.com/articles/jetstar-just-did-australian-travellers-a-favour-but-nobody-wants-to-hear-it%20)).
- 2026-08-08 — Social media comments are angry and sarcastic, e.g. asking whether a toilet fee is next ([Travel Radar, 8 Aug 2026](https://travelradar.aero/jetstar-introduces-controversial-carry-on-charges/)).
- 2026-08-10 — Coverage reports users accusing Jetstar of dressing a revenue move up as a service improvement, one calling it "a masterclass in gaslighting" ([IBTimes UK, 10 Aug 2026](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/jetstar-overhead-bin-fees-global-airline-changes-1813248)).

## Overall reaction

Negative. Most passengers quoted, the opposition and social media treated it as a revenue grab. The government asked for clear disclosure but did not oppose it. A minority, including AirlineRatings and some passengers, saw benefits in dropping the weight limit and gate weighing.

## What people objected to

- A revenue grab presented as a service improvement.
- Yet another add-on fee on top of paid food, seats and checked bags, and doubts about transparency of the total price.
- Families with young children need overhead space and cannot check everything.
- It narrows the price gap with Qantas, which includes bags and a meal.
- Prices vary by route and may change with demand.

## Who pushed back

- Passengers and social media users.
- The federal opposition (Senator Bridget McKenzie).
- Families travelling with children; a former flight attendant.
- The Transport Minister asked for fee disclosure without opposing the policy.

## Did they back down?

No. No change or delay was reported in the sources checked (to 10 August 2026); the fee is due to start in February 2027.
