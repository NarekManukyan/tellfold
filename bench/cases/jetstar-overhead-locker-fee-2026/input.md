# Jetstar will charge passengers to put a cabin bag in the overhead locker from February 2027

## Who the body is

Jetstar is the low-cost airline of the Qantas Group, flying domestic routes in Australia and international routes such as Perth to Bali and Cairns to Tokyo. Its fares today include cabin baggage up to 7 kg, which staff may weigh at the boarding gate, and passengers pay extra for checked bags, seat selection, meals and entertainment. Australian and New Zealand airlines already charge for checked bags and some extras, but none charges specifically for overhead locker space. Earlier in 2026 Qantas began selling Economy Plus seats that include priority boarding and first access to the lockers; Virgin Australia has offered a similar paid product since 2017.

## What was announced

On Wednesday 5 August 2026 Jetstar said that, from February 2027:

- Every fare will include one personal item that fits under the seat in front (for example a backpack, handbag or laptop bag), up to about 40 x 30 x 20 cm. The 7 kg weight limit on cabin bags is dropped and staff will no longer routinely weigh bags at the gate.
- Passengers who want a larger bag (up to 56 x 36 x 23 cm, and under about 10 kg so it can be lifted safely) in the overhead locker must buy a new add-on called **Priority Carry-on**, which also includes early boarding.
- Priority Carry-on starts at A$25 one way on short domestic routes, for example Launceston to Sydney, about A$39 for Perth to Bali and about A$52 for Cairns to Tokyo. Prices vary by route and flight.
- Infants get an extra under-seat bag for baby items at no charge.
- Checked baggage is unchanged and can still be bought up to 40 kg per passenger.

## Stated reasons

- Passengers and crew told Jetstar that having bags weighed at the gate and fighting for locker space were among the most stressful parts of flying.
- Charging for the lockers should make better use of the space, speed up boarding and help more flights leave on time.
- Customers only pay for what they need, so travelling with less costs less, which helps keep base fares low.
- The airline says the model matches European low-cost carriers such as Ryanair, easyJet and Wizz Air.

## How it was communicated

A Jetstar statement with comments from chief executive Stephanie Tully, followed by news coverage the same day.
