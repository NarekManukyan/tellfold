# Rebel Wolves confirms The Blood of Dawnwalker will run at up to 40fps on consoles

## Who the body is

Rebel Wolves is a Polish studio making The Blood of Dawnwalker, a large open-world action role-playing game about a vampire, built on Unreal Engine 5 and published by Bandai Namco. It is the studio's first game. It releases on 3 September 2026 on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. In recent years most big console releases have offered a "performance" mode targeting 60 frames per second next to a higher-resolution "quality" mode at 30fps, and the PS5 Pro is sold partly on running games more smoothly.

## What was announced

On Friday 21 August 2026, under two weeks before launch, Rebel Wolves released a video of console gameplay that set out the graphics modes for PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X:

- **Quality mode:** targets 30fps at a dynamic 4K resolution. This is the default.
- **Balanced mode:** targets up to 40fps at a dynamic 4K resolution. It needs a 120Hz display to work as intended, because 40 frames do not divide evenly into a 60Hz refresh.
- **PS5 Pro:** the same frame-rate targets as the base PS5, with a narrower resolution range for a sharper image.
- **No 60fps option:** the video stated that 40fps is the maximum on all console hardware.

PC frame rates depend on the player's hardware.

## Stated reasons

The video did not give a specific reason for the frame-rate ceiling. The modes put the emphasis on resolution and visual detail in a large open world. All figures are described as targets.

## How it was communicated

A console gameplay video and accompanying posts on the studio's channels, answering players' questions about console performance ahead of release, followed by press write-ups over the weekend.
