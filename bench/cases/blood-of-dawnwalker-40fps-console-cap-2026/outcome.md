# Outcome: The Blood of Dawnwalker's 40fps console cap

## Timeline

- 2026-08-21 (Friday) — Rebel Wolves publishes console gameplay and graphics modes: 30fps Quality and up to 40fps Balanced on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X, with no 60fps option (day per the studio's later post quoted by Kotaku and The Sixth Axis; [Inven Global, 23 Aug 2026](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/25042/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-console-graphics-modes-confirmed-max-frame-rate-capped-at-40-fps)).
- 2026-08-23 — Inven Global reports players disappointed straight after the reveal, especially that even the PS5 Pro gets no 60fps mode, against the recent norm of a 60fps console option ([Inven Global, 23 Aug 2026](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/25042/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-console-graphics-modes-confirmed-max-frame-rate-capped-at-40-fps)).
- 2026-08-24 — Kotaku reports many players unhappy that no 60fps mode was mentioned, with one YouTube commenter saying they would buy on PC instead. The same day Rebel Wolves posts on X that a Performance Mode targeting 60fps will come to PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X in the day-one patch: it had been in development but still in testing, and "We heard your feedback" ([Kotaku, 24 Aug 2026](https://kotaku.com/vampire-rpg-blood-of-dawnwalker-will-rush-out-a-60fps-mode-after-after-backlash-we-heard-your-feedback-2000727511)). The Sixth Axis describes the original announcement as followed by an "outcry" and the response as "light crisis management"; Xbox Series S stays without a 60fps mode ([The Sixth Axis, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/08/24/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-will-get-a-60fps-performance-mode-in-day-one-patch/)).
- 2026-09-03 — Launch date, with the 60fps mode promised via the day-one patch.

## Overall reaction

Negative among console players. The complaint was simple and widely shared: a 2026 action game with no 60fps option, even on the PS5 Pro, and a 40fps mode that needs a 120Hz screen. Some players said they would switch to the PC version. The studio added a 60fps mode within three days, and coverage reported relief, with remaining doubts about why it had not been ready and how stable it would be.

## What people objected to

- No 60fps mode on any console, including the PS5 Pro.
- Falling short of the now-standard 60fps console option.
- The best console mode needing a 120Hz display.
- Some console buyers saying they would buy the PC version instead.

## Who pushed back

- Console players, especially PS5 Pro owners.
- Commenters on YouTube and social media under the gameplay video.

## Did they back down?

Yes, fully on the main demand. On 24 August Rebel Wolves announced a 60fps Performance Mode for PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X in the day-one patch. Xbox Series S was left at 30fps, and all modes remain targets rather than guarantees.
