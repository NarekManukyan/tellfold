# CD Projekt Red announces The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered as a free upgrade

*Benchmark seed: a neutral summary of the announcement as it stood on 2026-08-25, written for the Tellfold accuracy test. Not the original text.*

## Who is announcing

CD Projekt Red (CDPR) is a Polish game studio, known for The Witcher series and Cyberpunk 2077. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2015), with its two expansions Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine, has sold tens of millions of copies. It got a free "next-gen" update in 2022. The studio is currently making The Witcher 4. In recent years remasters of older games from many publishers have often been sold as new products, typically for $40–50, or with a paid upgrade of $10 or more for existing owners.

## What was announced

At Gamescom Opening Night Live on 25 August 2026, CDPR revealed **The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Remastered**, releasing on **29 September 2026** on **PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2** (the game's first native Switch 2 release).

- **Price for existing owners:** a **free upgrade** for everyone who owns The Witcher 3 on supported platforms. Owners of the original Switch version get the Switch 2 version free. Owners who do not have the expansions can also get Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine with it at no cost.
- **Graphics:** an overhaul with new textures, a new shading model and tone mapper, and improved ray and path tracing on PC. Upscaling support includes DLSS 4.5, FSR 4 and XeSS 2.0, plus PS5 Pro support.
- **Gameplay:** reworked combat (new camera and targeting, new finishers, new effects for Witcher signs), improved monster behaviour, adjusted movement, climbing and horse handling, a redesigned skill tree, transmog (an item's stats with another's look), a new quest tracker, a looting rework and an expanded photo mode.
- **Other:** cross-platform mod support and Xbox Play Anywhere.
- **New expansion:** a third expansion, **Songs of the Past**, co-developed with Fool's Theory, is planned for 2027. CDPR showed a short teaser.

## How it was communicated

A trailer and segment during Gamescom Opening Night Live on 25 August 2026, followed by CDPR's official channels and press coverage the same evening.
