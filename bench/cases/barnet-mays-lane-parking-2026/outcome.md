# Outcome: Barnet Council scales back Mays Lane parking charges

## Timeline

- 2026-01-30 — (Background) Traders say trade is down by up to 40% since pay-by-phone charges replaced free parking in mid-December 2025; a petition run from the shops has more than 1,500 signatures ([Barnet Society, 30 Jan 2026](https://www.barnetsociety.org.uk/despair-among-mays-lane-traders-as-introduction-of-pay-by-phone-parking-charges-leads-to-a-dramatic-loss-in-customers)).
- 2026-07-23 — Council makes an experimental order cutting the charged period in the Mays Lane bays to 2pm–3pm on weekdays from 3 August, citing feedback since the bays were introduced ([Public Notice Portal / Barnet Borough Times, 23 Jul 2026](https://publicnoticeportal.uk/notice/traffic-and-roads/6a635675f0130fae7d1e46cc)).
- 2026-08-03 — New hours take effect; a six-month consultation runs to 3 February 2027 ([Barnet Society, 7 Aug 2026](https://www.barnetsociety.org.uk/reprieve-for-mays-lane-traders-as-barnet-council-lifts-all-day-parking-charges-outside-their-parade-of-shops/)).
- 2026-08-07 — Traders welcome the change. One shop owner says he is "very pleased" and credits the residents' groups that lobbied the council; a pet shop worker calls the one-hour charge a step in the right direction. Both say they are still worried because it is only a trial, and fear shoppers may still be put off by the risk of a penalty ticket ([Barnet Society, 7 Aug 2026](https://www.barnetsociety.org.uk/reprieve-for-mays-lane-traders-as-barnet-council-lifts-all-day-parking-charges-outside-their-parade-of-shops/)).

## Overall reaction

Positive. The people who had campaigned against the charges (traders, two residents' associations, petition signers) welcomed the change. Reservations were about permanence, not the change itself: it is an 18-month experiment, and traders worried customers would stay away for fear of fines. No group was reported opposing the reduced charges.

## What people objected to

- It is only a trial and could be reversed after the experiment.
- Some shoppers may still avoid the parade for fear of penalty charge notices.
- Traders had wanted free short stays all day; one paid hour remains.

## Who pushed back

No group pushed back against the change. The groups that had pressed for it were Mays Lane traders, the Quinta Village Green Residents Association and the Underhill Residents Group. A separate, unresolved dispute continued over the proposed Underhill South CPZ, with a council decision expected by the end of December 2026.

## Did they back down?

Not applicable in the usual sense: the announcement was itself a climb-down from the December 2025 charges. The scaled-back hours took effect on 3 August 2026 as announced and were not reversed in the period checked.
