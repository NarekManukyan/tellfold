# Outcome: OpenAI halves ChatGPT Pro 200 allowance (announced 2026-09-29)

## Timeline

- 2026-09-29 — At DevDay, OpenAI launches Pro 500 and reduces Pro 200 from 20x to 10x Plus usage, effective 30 October for existing subscribers. A forum user quoted the same day says they will give it one more week before moving everything to Claude ([Jutsu](https://jutsu.ai/blog/openai-halved-chatgpt-pro-ai-usage-governance)).
- 2026-09-30 — MakeUseOf describes the 62,500-credit grant as damage control after users complained about the plan changes and about weekly limits running out within hours ([MakeUseOf](https://www.makeuseof.com/openai-is-giving-away-62500-free-credits-to-pro/)).
- 2026-10-01 — Trending Topics calls it a "hidden price hike": every tier now costs $20 per Plus-equivalent unit, so the old Pro 200 volume discount is gone ([Trending Topics](https://trendingtopics.eu/chatgpt-pro-tokens-halved/)).
- 2026-10-02 — PCWorld reports that OpenAI's explanations are not going over well with the ChatGPT community and that some users cancelled in protest ([PCWorld](https://pcworld.com/article/3249113/chatgpt-pros-biggest-perk-just-got-cut-in-half-meet-the-new-ai-subscription-math.html)).
- 2026-10-29 — OpenAI reverses course. Its Pro help page says the reduction announced in the 29 September email is cancelled. OpenAI's Tibo (Codex/ChatGPT) posts that usage limits are doubled for all Pro 100/200/500 tiers and Business Premium, effective immediately, citing users' "kind feedback" ([BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-doubles-chatgpt-pro-usage-limits-after-backlash-over-planned-50-percent-cut); [Mixed](https://mixed-news.com/en/?p=39007)).
- 2026-10-30 — Coverage of the reversal. Reports differ on whether Pro 200 users got a true doubling or only avoided the cut. OpenAI published no exact numbers ([iTechify](https://itechify.com/?p=11808)).

## Overall reaction

Negative among Pro 200 subscribers and the developer community. People saw it as a price rise in disguise and a broken expectation for existing customers. Some commentators were more sympathetic, calling the change "essentially a wash" once API price cuts are counted and an inevitable correction for flat-rate AI plans (PCWorld; Trending Topics).

## What people objected to

- Same $200 price for about half the usage is a hidden price rise ([Trending Topics](https://trendingtopics.eu/chatgpt-pro-tokens-halved/)).
- The cut hits existing paying subscribers, and weekly limits already ran out within hours for heavy users ([MakeUseOf](https://www.makeuseof.com/openai-is-giving-away-62500-free-credits-to-pro/)).
- Codex coding usage is halved, and the most wanted model (GPT-6 Astra) did not get cheaper, so Astra users effectively lose half their tokens ([Jutsu](https://jutsu.ai/blog/openai-halved-chatgpt-pro-ai-usage-governance)).
- OpenAI's justifications did not convince users, and some cancelled ([PCWorld](https://pcworld.com/article/3249113/chatgpt-pros-biggest-perk-just-got-cut-in-half-meet-the-new-ai-subscription-math.html)).
- Some heavy users threatened to move to Anthropic's Claude plans ([Jutsu](https://jutsu.ai/blog/openai-halved-chatgpt-pro-ai-usage-governance)).

## Who pushed back

Existing Pro 200 subscribers, especially developers using Codex for long agentic coding sessions, plus AI power users on forums and X, and tech press.

## Did they back down?

Yes, fully. On 29 October, the day before the cut was due, OpenAI cancelled the Pro 200 reduction and said it was doubling usage limits across all Pro tiers and Business Premium. The new Pro 500 tier stayed. Uncertainty: OpenAI never published exact limits, so the size of the "doubling" cannot be checked.
