# Outcome: Sony ends physical discs for new PlayStation games (announced 2026-07-01)

## Timeline

- 2026-07-01 — Sony announces on the PlayStation Blog that disc production for new PlayStation games ends in January 2028 ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/07/01/physical-disc-production-ending-in-january-2028-for-new-games-releasing-on-playstation-consoles); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/sony-ends-physical-playstation-game-discs-2028)).
- 2026-07-02 — Coverage of a wave of criticism across PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo player communities. One critical X post passes about 18 million views and 25,000 comments, nearly all negative ([LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/playstations-move-to-digital-only-games-ignites-gamer-fury-blaming-company-for-ruining-industry-and-video-game-preservation/)).
- 2026-07-27 — The preservation group Does It Play? promotes a week-long #PSBlackout boycott for 23–30 August: consoles off, no purchases ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/PSBlackout-Major-August-boycott-aims-to-stop-Sony-from-ditching-discs.1352587.0.html)).
- 2026-07-31 — On Sony's earnings call, CFO Lin Tao acknowledges the strength of feeling but says Sony will "cautiously move this forward". The only accommodation mentioned is that publishers can still sell boxes containing a download code ([Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/games/339903/sony-responds-to-backlash-over-playstations-physical-disc-future); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/sony-defends-decision-to-end-playstation-discs-after-fan-backlash-3393470/)).
- 2026-08-23 to 2026-08-30 — #PSBlackout runs. PSN stays online, and commentators doubt it will have a measurable effect against more than 120 million users ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/PSBlackout-Major-August-boycott-aims-to-stop-Sony-from-ditching-discs.1352587.0.html)).
- 2026-09-03 — During the State of Play broadcast, viewers fill the chat with "no disc, no buy", drowning out discussion of the actual game announcements ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/the-recent-state-of-play-should-be-sonys-wake-up-call-to-double-back-on-the-digital-only-future)).
- As of 2026-09-08 — Sony has made no change. Commentators call a reversal "very unlikely" ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/the-recent-state-of-play-should-be-sonys-wake-up-call-to-double-back-on-the-digital-only-future)).

## Overall reaction

Strongly negative among vocal players and preservation groups from day one. It stayed negative for months, with an organised boycott and protests during Sony's own broadcasts. A minority of commenters pointed out that physical media was already declining across the industry, and analysts saw the move as an expected step.

## What people objected to

- Without discs, players stop owning their games: no reselling, lending or trading ([LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/playstations-move-to-digital-only-games-ignites-gamer-fury-blaming-company-for-ruining-industry-and-video-game-preservation/)).
- Game preservation: digital-only titles can disappear when servers or licences end ([LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/playstations-move-to-digital-only-games-ignites-gamer-fury-blaming-company-for-ruining-industry-and-video-game-preservation/)).
- Harm to the used-game market and independent retailers ([LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/playstations-move-to-digital-only-games-ignites-gamer-fury-blaming-company-for-ruining-industry-and-video-game-preservation/)).
- Boxes with download codes are no real substitute, because a code cannot be resold once claimed ([Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/games/339903/sony-responds-to-backlash-over-playstations-physical-disc-future)).
- Some long-time players said PlayStation would have no edge over PC without physical games, and threatened to switch ([LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/playstations-move-to-digital-only-games-ignites-gamer-fury-blaming-company-for-ruining-industry-and-video-game-preservation/)).

## Who pushed back

Physical-media collectors, game-preservation groups (notably Does It Play?), long-time PlayStation owners, people who buy and sell used games, and players from other console communities. A Change.org petition circulated ([Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/games/339903/sony-responds-to-backlash-over-playstations-physical-disc-future)).

## Did they back down?

No. Sony restated the plan on 31 July and said it would proceed cautiously. Its only clarification was that boxed retail products with download codes can still exist. No change had been reported as of early September 2026.
