# Sony to end physical disc production for new PlayStation games from January 2028

## Who is announcing

Sony Interactive Entertainment runs the PlayStation games business: the PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4 consoles, the PlayStation Store and the PlayStation Network. The PS5 has shipped close to 94 million units and the network reports around 125 million monthly active users. The PS5 is sold in two versions: a standard model with a disc drive and a cheaper Digital Edition without one. Games have been sold both as discs in retail shops and as downloads from the PlayStation Store.

Sony's own financial reports show downloads already account for most full-game sales on PS4 and PS5: about 78% across the last fiscal year and around 85% in the latest quarter. Physical game retail has been shrinking in general. GameStop, for example, went from roughly 2,900 US stores in early 2024 to about 1,600 by January 2026.

## What was announced

On 1 July 2026, in a post on the official PlayStation Blog from its content communications team, Sony said:

- From **January 2028**, it will stop producing physical discs for **new** games releasing on PlayStation consoles.
- After that date, new PlayStation games will be sold only in digital form, through the PlayStation Store and through retailers.
- Games already released on disc, and games that come out on disc before January 2028, are not affected. Existing discs keep working on consoles that have a disc drive.
- Sony says it will keep offering players a choice of where to buy new games, at retailers or on the PlayStation Store, and will keep investing in new ways to access games.

**Stated reasons:** Sony says consumer preferences, and the wider entertainment industry, have moved from physical media to digital. It says the general preference for digital is now well ahead of physical discs and that the change matches how most of its community already plays.

On the same day Sony also published a separate update about the older PlayStation Store for PS3 and PS Vita, which will begin closing in some markets from August 2026 and close worldwide in July 2027. Sony says previously purchased content will stay downloadable for the foreseeable future.

## How it was communicated

A PlayStation Blog post on 1 July 2026, picked up by games and technology news sites the same day. The post did not give details on retail packaging after 2028 or say whether future consoles will include disc drives.
