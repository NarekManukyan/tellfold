# Outcome: World of Warcraft: Forever and the paid Skyborne race

## Overall reaction

Mixed. The game itself was the long-requested "Classic+" and drew real excitement, with lapsed players saying they would return. But the paid Skyborne race became the main talking point within a day: players and several outlets objected to locking a race and starting zone behind a $30 pack, to beta access costing $60, and to modern-looking additions in a version sold on nostalgia. Both sides were loudly reported.

## What people objected to

- A playable race and starting zone sold separately on top of a subscription ("cash elves").
- Beta access tied to the $59.99 pack, turning excitement into purchase pressure.
- No timeline for the Skyborne to become part of the normal subscription.
- Microtransactions and modern-looking additions (the race, new graphics, surnames) in a version meant to feel like the original.
- The choice of yet another elf race nobody asked for.

## Who pushed back

- Classic and "Classic+" purists on Reddit and forums.
- Players opposed to microtransactions in a subscription game.
- Commentators at FinalBoss, MeinMMO and Massively OP.

Supporters: long-time and lapsed WoW players who had asked for Classic+ for years.

## Did they back down?

No. The Skyborne stayed behind the paid packs through launch. In late October Blizzard rebalanced racial abilities, including the Skyborne's, but that was game tuning, not a change to how the race is sold (search snippet only; [Icy Veins, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/final-racial-tuning-ahead-of-launch-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-october-26th/)).
