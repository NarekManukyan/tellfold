# Blizzard announces World of Warcraft: Forever, with a new Skyborne race sold in paid packs

## Who the body is

Blizzard Entertainment makes World of Warcraft (WoW), a subscription online role-playing game first released in 2004. Today Blizzard runs the modern game alongside "Classic" versions that recreate older eras of WoW. For years part of the Classic audience has asked for a "Classic+": the original game extended with new content rather than replayed as it was. BlizzCon, Blizzard's fan convention, returned on 12-13 September 2026 in Anaheim, its first edition since 2023.

## What was announced

At the BlizzCon opening ceremony on Saturday 12 September 2026, Blizzard revealed **World of Warcraft: Forever**, a third version of WoW that sits next to the modern and Classic games.

- **What it is:** a version built on the original 2004-era game, with a level cap of 60 and the world itself as the focus rather than one big heroic story. Planned additions over time include new zones, more than 1,000 new quests, more dungeons and raids, new professions and reputations, better PvP, updated visuals and a new race.
- **Options:** players can switch off many of the modern touches, such as new lighting, fog and water effects, transmog and quest map guidance.
- **Access:** WoW: Forever is included in the standard WoW subscription or game time, with no separate box purchase.
- **New race:** the Skyborne, a new elf race open to both factions, with its own starting island, Zephras Isle (levels 1-12). Playing a Skyborne requires one of three paid editions:
  - Skyborne Heroic Pack, $29.99 (£25.99): the race and its starting zone, early name reservation, a mount and cosmetics.
  - Skyborne Epic Pack, $59.99 (£51.99): adds beta access and 30 days of game time.
  - Warcraft Forever Collection (£69.99): adds Warcraft III: Reforged with a new campaign.
  No way to unlock the race after launch without one of these was listed.
- **Dates:** beta from 17 September for Epic Pack and Collection buyers; launch on 4 November 2026. BlizzCon attendees could play it on the show floor.

## Stated reasons

- Blizzard described Forever as a new way to play that is "friendly, familiar, and new", built for players who love the original game.
- Designers said elves are extremely popular, that a new elf race shifted faction balance in the past, and that the Skyborne draw on unfinished story material from the original game.

## How it was communicated

A cinematic trailer and segment in the BlizzCon opening ceremony, a deep-dive panel the next day, a playable demo on the show floor and pre-purchase pages listing the editions.
