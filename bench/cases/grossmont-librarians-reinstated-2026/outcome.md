# Outcome: Grossmont brings back its teacher-librarians

## Timeline

- 2026-10-21 — Settlement announced. The GEA president calls it a "testament to the power of solidarity" and to the union's commitment to students; the report recalls the student, staff and community protests of 2025, private board text threads that raised open-meetings questions, and a pending lawsuit by cut librarians ([KPBS, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.kpbs.org/news/education/2026/10/21/grossmont-union-high-school-district-to-reinstate-librarian-positions)).
- 2026-10-21 — A former Mt. Miguel High librarian calls the reinstatement "a great victory" for the community's combined efforts; the union president says educators "decided enough was enough". The report notes only nine of 49 cut posts return, with no back pay, and that the union is campaigning against sitting board members ([Times of San Diego, 21 Oct 2026](https://timesofsandiego.com/?p=406322)).

## Overall reaction

Positive. The people who had fought the 2025 cuts (the teachers' union, former librarians and, earlier, students and parents who protested) treated the settlement as a win. No one was reported objecting to bringing the librarians back. The caveats were about what the deal leaves out: most cut positions stay cut, the return is a year away, there is no back pay, and lawsuits and questions about the board's conduct continue.

## What people objected to

Little objection to the decision itself. Limitations and concerns raised:

- Only the nine teacher-librarians return, not the other 40 student-facing posts cut in 2025.
- Librarians do not return until 2027-28 and get no back pay or damages.
- It took a formal labour charge and over a year of protest; questions remain about how the board majority made the cuts, including private text threads.
- Lawsuits by former staff are still pending against the district.

## Who pushed back

No group pushed back against the reinstatement. The union and former librarians welcomed it while continuing to criticise the board majority, and the union is campaigning against sitting board members.

## Did they back down?

No. The settlement stands as announced.
