# Xbox introduces monthly hour allowances for cloud gaming on Game Pass

## Who is announcing

Microsoft's Xbox division runs Xbox Game Pass, a subscription service with three tiers: Essential, Premium and Ultimate. It gives members a rotating library of games to download and play on Xbox consoles and PC. Xbox Cloud Gaming lets members stream many of those games instead of downloading them, to phones, tablets, smart TVs, browsers, cheaper hardware and consoles. Until now, streaming has been included in eligible Game Pass tiers with no time limit. Microsoft has promoted cloud gaming as part of a strategy of letting people play Xbox games on any screen.

## What was announced

On 3 September 2026, in a post on the official Xbox blog, Microsoft announced that from **November 2026** cloud gaming on Game Pass will have **monthly playtime allowances**:

- **Game Pass Ultimate:** 15 hours of cloud gaming per month
- **Game Pass Premium:** 10 hours per month
- **Game Pass Essential:** 5 hours per month

Other details:

- Members who use up their allowance will be able to buy extra cloud playtime through the Xbox Store. Pricing will be shared in November.
- For the first time, people **without** a Game Pass subscription will also be able to buy cloud playtime and stream eligible games.
- Downloading and playing games locally on console or PC is **not** affected and stays unlimited.
- Microsoft expects the change to affect about **4%** of Game Pass subscribers, the ones who stream the most.

**Stated reasons:** Microsoft said the cost of running cloud gaming grows as more people use it and play for longer. It said the allowances will help fund performance and reliability. Microsoft acknowledged this is a meaningful change, and that for some heavy streaming players the practical result will be a higher cost.

## How it was communicated

A post on the Xbox blog on 3 September 2026, with notices to Game Pass members to follow.
