# Outcome: RuneScape Reignited

## Timeline

- 2026-10-03 — Jagex announces RuneScape Reignited at RuneFest, due 2 December, with fresh characters, a separate economy and new high scores ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape_Reignited)).
- Between 3 and 6 October (no exact dates reported) — Long-time players object loudly, mostly on social media, that the mode would split the player base and shrink the veteran population ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape_Reignited); [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79372-jagex-cancels-runescape-reignited-following-community-feedback)).
- 2026-10-06 — Jagex says it is "re-evaluating RuneScape Reignited" ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape_Reignited)).
- 2026-10-09 — Mod Ryan's update floats concessions: letting existing accounts and memberships start Reignited characters, carrying veteran rewards over, and limiting or dropping Reignited-only worlds ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape_Reignited)).
- 2026-10-15 — Jagex cancels Reignited. The team says "we missed the mark", cites feedback after RuneFest, recalls that ignoring players once changed combat for a decade, and says growth must never come at the existing community's expense. The Adventure Guide, remastered journey and free trial still go ahead on 2 December ([WorthPlaying, 15 Oct 2026](http://worthplaying.com/article/2026/10/15/news/151257-runescape-reignited-update-canceled-mere-days-after-being-announced-trailer/)).
- 2026-10-16 — Kotaku reports veterans "manage to kill" the mode in two weeks; players argued it favoured newcomers over veterans and that fixing current problems would do more than wiping the board ([Kotaku, 16 Oct 2026](https://kotaku.com/runescape-announces-then-quickly-cancels-its-reignited-project-due-to-player-feedback-2000746746)).

## Overall reaction

Negative among the existing RuneScape community, who are the game's core audience. Veterans saw Reignited as a rival fresh-start game that would pull new and returning players away from the main world and devalue their years of progress. Jagex first offered concessions, then cancelled the mode 12 days after announcing it.

## What people objected to

- It would split the player base and shrink the veteran population.
- It catered to newcomers at the expense of long-time players.
- It would become the default choice for new and returning players, draining the main game.
- Existing accounts and progress could not carry over.
- Jagex should fix the game's current problems instead of starting over.

## Who pushed back

- Long-time RuneScape (RS3) players, many with thousands of hours and money invested.
- The community on social media and forums.

## Did they back down?

Yes, fully. After saying on 6 October it was re-evaluating and floating changes on 9 October, Jagex cancelled RuneScape Reignited on 15 October 2026. The other new-player updates went ahead as planned.
