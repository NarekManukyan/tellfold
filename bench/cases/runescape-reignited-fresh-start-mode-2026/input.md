# Jagex announces RuneScape Reignited, a fresh-start mode with its own economy

## Who the body is

Jagex is the UK developer of RuneScape (often called RS3), an online role-playing game that has run for about 25 years, alongside its sister game Old School RuneScape. Jagex has said it wants RuneScape to grow again by attracting new and returning players. RuneFest is Jagex's annual fan event, where it sets out plans for the coming year.

## What was announced

At RuneFest on Saturday 3 October 2026, Jagex announced **RuneScape Reignited**, due on 2 December 2026, so that new players could experience the game "as if it was released for the first time":

- **New characters only:** a Reignited character starts from scratch on a new account; existing characters cannot be converted (described as subject to change).
- **Separate economy:** Reignited characters trade through their own Grand Exchange and cannot trade with existing ("veteran") characters or join them in boss fights.
- **Shared worlds:** Reignited and veteran characters can be on the same worlds, chat, form clans and share player housing, and there will also be Reignited-only worlds.
- **Fresh records:** separate high-score tables, with world-first achievements reset and the first players to reach key milestones honoured with statues in a Hall of Legends.
- **Store limits:** Reignited characters cannot buy certain store items.
- **No merge planned:** there were no immediate plans to merge Reignited characters into the main economy, though this was not ruled out.

Announced alongside it for the same date: an Adventure Guide for new players, the first part of a remastered early-game journey, and a ten-day free membership trial for new and lapsed players (2-12 December). The 2027 roadmap includes combat and skilling content, a grandmaster quest and an Agility rework.

## Stated reasons

- To give new and returning players a level starting point and a reason to try RuneScape now.
- To grow the game's population again.

## How it was communicated

A presentation on the RuneFest stage, streamed online, followed by posts on the RuneScape website and social channels.
