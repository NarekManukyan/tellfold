# Samsung launches a wider "passport" Galaxy Z Fold 8, a Fold 8 Ultra and the Z Flip 8, each $100 dearer

## Who the body is

Samsung Electronics is the largest maker of foldable smartphones. It launches new foldables every summer at a Galaxy Unpacked event. Last year's Galaxy Z Fold 7 was made much thinner and dropped support for the S Pen stylus. In 2026 memory chip prices have risen sharply, phone makers are raising prices, and Apple is widely expected to release its first foldable iPhone in the autumn.

## What was announced

At Galaxy Unpacked in London on Wednesday 22 July 2026, Samsung announced three foldables for the first time instead of two:

- **Galaxy Z Fold 8 (from $1,899.99, 12 GB / 256 GB):** a new wider, shorter "passport" shape with a 7.6-inch inner screen in a 4:3 ratio and a 5.5-inch wide cover screen. It weighs 201 g, the lightest Fold so far, and is 4.5 mm thin unfolded. It has a 4,800 mAh battery, 45 W charging, 50 MP main and 50 MP ultrawide cameras (no telephoto), the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip and IP48 water resistance. It does not support the S Pen.
- **Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra (from $2,099.99):** a larger 8-inch inner screen, 200 MP main camera, 5,000 mAh battery and 8K video, aimed at productivity. No S Pen support either.
- **Galaxy Z Flip 8 (from $1,199.99, 256 GB):** a slimmer, lighter flip phone with a redesigned cover screen.
- Each model starts $100 higher than its predecessor. Pre-orders opened the same day with trade-in credits and bundled subscriptions; general sales begin 7 August.
- Also announced: Galaxy Watch 9 and Watch Ultra 2, Gemini-powered "agentic" AI features across apps, file sharing to Apple devices via Quick Share, and a teaser for Galaxy smart glasses.

## Stated reasons

- Samsung called it its most important foldable launch and said the three shapes serve different buyers: media and reading (Fold 8), work (Fold 8 Ultra) and style and portability (Flip 8).
- Samsung said S Pen use on earlier Folds was low.
- Higher component costs, particularly memory, were cited for the higher prices.

## How it was communicated

A live-streamed Unpacked keynote, Samsung newsroom posts and press briefings, with hands-on demos for media at the event.
