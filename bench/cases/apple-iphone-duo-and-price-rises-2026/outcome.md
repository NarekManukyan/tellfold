# Outcome: Apple iPhone Duo launch and iPhone price rises

## Timeline

- 2026-09-09 — Apple raises the iPhone 16, 17e, 17 and Air by $100 on its online store as it launches the iPhone 18 Pro. The most-upvoted reader comments on MacRumors attack the increase, for example that a two-year-old iPhone 16 still costing $800 is "crazy" ([MacRumors, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/apple-raises-iphone-17-prices/)).
- 2026-09-09 — UK site NerdZap calls the Duo's price "a genuinely good outcome" for people who had been waiting, and notes the Pro's rise came in below analyst forecasts ([NerdZap, 9 Sep 2026](https://nerdzap.com/news/apple-iphone-duo-18-pro-event-reaction/)).
- 2026-09-10 — Counterpoint calls pricing the Duo only $100 above the Galaxy Z Fold 8 a "bold move" and forecasts up to six million foldable iPhones this year; an eMarketer analyst says the price will test loyal customers; members of the public interviewed are lukewarm ("at $2,000 I just don't know") ([The National, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/09/09/iphone-duo-price-apple-event/)).
- 2026-09-10 — M.G. Siegler says the Duo "looks great", calls it another potential hit and says he may buy one, while calling the pricing "decidedly less clever" ([Spyglass, 10 Sep 2026](https://spyglass.org/iphone-duo-event/)).
- 2026-09-15 — John Gruber says the Duo is "expensive" and "double-priced" against the Pro, calls missing Face ID a drawback that could hurt the first model, but judges the Pro price increases not too bad given costs ([Daring Fireball, 15 Sep 2026](https://daringfireball.net/2026/09/thoughts_and_observations_on_apples_surprise_and_shine_event)).

## Overall reaction

Mixed. The iPhone Duo drew curiosity and broadly favourable first impressions from commentators and analysts, who found its $1,999 starting price lower than feared and close to Samsung's. Against that, the price rises were the main complaint: $100 more for the Pro models and, more pointedly, $100 more for older iPhones already on sale, which readers on Apple enthusiast sites treated as a cash grab. Some commentators and members of the public doubted they would pay about $2,000 for a foldable.

## What people objected to

- Raising prices on existing, older iPhones (an iPhone 16 at $799 two years after launch).
- The $100 rise on the iPhone 18 Pro, and steeper rises on higher storage tiers and outside the US.
- The Duo's high price, especially relative to the Pro, and storage costs at the top end.
- Duo compromises: no Face ID and no dedicated telephoto camera.

## Who pushed back

- Apple enthusiasts and readers on sites such as MacRumors, mostly over the older-iPhone price rises.
- Some analysts (eMarketer) and commentators (Daring Fireball) over Duo pricing and features.
- Ordinary buyers unconvinced by a $2,000 foldable.

Supporters included analysts at Counterpoint and CCS Insight and several Apple commentators who praised the Duo's design.

## Did they back down?

No. No price change or reversal was reported in the sources checked; the Duo launched on its announced schedule as far as the sources show.
