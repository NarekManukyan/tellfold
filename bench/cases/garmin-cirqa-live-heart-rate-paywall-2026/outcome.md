# Outcome: Garmin CIRQA live heart rate behind Connect+ (launched 2026-07-21)

## Timeline

- 2026-07-21 — Garmin launches CIRQA at $199.99, marketed as needing no subscription for its advanced features ([The Gadgeteer](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/07/21/garmin-cirqa-smart-band/); [Marathon Handbook](https://marathonhandbook.com/garmin-cirqa-screenless-band/)).
- 2026-07-21/22 — Early hands-on reviews, led by Ray Maker (DC Rainmaker), point out that seeing live heart rate during a workout requires the $6.99/month Connect+ subscription. Critics say this contradicts the "no subscription" pitch ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/garmin-cirqa-live-heart-rate-3690344/); [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Garmin-CIRQA-will-get-basic-feature-without-Connect-subscription-following-outcry.1349333.0.html)).
- 2026-07-22 — Garmin confirms to Maker that a Connect app update "in the coming days" will show live heart rate to non-subscribers ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/garmin-cirqa-live-heart-rate-3690344/); [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Garmin-CIRQA-will-get-basic-feature-without-Connect-subscription-following-outcry.1349333.0.html)).
- 2026-07-23 — Gizmodo reports the change. Other Connect+ features stay paid: calorie tracking, nutrition recommendations, wheelchair workouts and Garmin Coach plans ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/garmins-whoop-killer-will-offer-free-heart-rate-monitoring-after-backlash-2000789941)).

## Overall reaction

Negative on the specific point of paywalled live heart rate, which reviewers and tech outlets saw as a basic feature hidden behind a subscription in a product sold as subscription-free. Android Authority's writer called it "pointedly greedy". Reaction to the hardware itself was more positive. The episode lasted about a day before Garmin conceded.

## What people objected to

- Live heart rate during a workout is a basic feature and should not need a subscription ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/garmin-cirqa-live-heart-rate-3690344/)).
- Gating it contradicts Garmin's "no subscription required" marketing for CIRQA ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Garmin-CIRQA-will-get-basic-feature-without-Connect-subscription-following-outcry.1349333.0.html)).
- Other in-workout displays (heart-rate zones, metric charts) and features such as calories, nutrition and coaching are also locked behind Connect+ ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Garmin-CIRQA-will-get-basic-feature-without-Connect-subscription-following-outcry.1349333.0.html); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/garmins-whoop-killer-will-offer-free-heart-rate-monitoring-after-backlash-2000789941)).

## Who pushed back

Fitness-tech reviewers with early units (notably DC Rainmaker), tech press (Android Authority, Notebookcheck, Gizmodo, TechRadar), and prospective buyers who chose CIRQA over Whoop specifically to avoid a subscription.

## Did they back down?

Partly, and very fast. Within about a day Garmin agreed to make live heart rate free for all CIRQA owners through an app update. The rest of the Connect+ paywall stayed, and it was unclear whether heart-rate zone graphs would be included ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Garmin-CIRQA-will-get-basic-feature-without-Connect-subscription-following-outcry.1349333.0.html)).
