# Garmin launches the CIRQA screen-free smart band

*Benchmark seed: a neutral summary of the announcement as it stood on 2026-07-21, written for the Tellfold accuracy test. Not the original text.*

## Who is announcing

Garmin makes GPS sports watches, fitness trackers and outdoor devices. Its customers range from serious runners, cyclists and triathletes to casual health trackers. All Garmin wearables sync to the free Garmin Connect app. In March 2025 Garmin added an optional paid tier, **Garmin Connect+** ($6.99 a month or $69.99 a year), with extra features such as AI-based insights, an advanced performance dashboard, nutrition tools and other premium coaching features. Core Garmin data has stayed free.

Screen-free fitness bands are a crowded category. Whoop sells its band only with a membership, priced from about $199 to $359 a year. Several rivals, including Amazfit, Polar and Google's Fitbit Air, sell screenless bands without a required subscription.

## What was announced

On 21 July 2026 Garmin announced the **CIRQA Smart Band**, its first screen-free band, available immediately at **$199.99** (US).

- A fabric strap with a small sensor pod and one button. It can be worn on the wrist or upper arm (a bicep strap is sold separately for $59). Seven colours, two sizes, water resistant to 50 m.
- Up to 10 days of battery life.
- Tracks heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep stages, stress, Body Battery, skin temperature, Pulse Ox (where available) and menstrual and pregnancy data.
- Uses the same training and recovery engine as Garmin's high-end watches: Training Readiness, Training Status, training load, recovery time and VO2 max.
- More than 80 activity types, started with the button or detected automatically. It uses the phone's GPS for routes, and can broadcast heart rate to other devices.
- With no screen, all data is shown in the Garmin Connect phone app.

**Subscription position:** Garmin markets CIRQA's advanced fitness and wellness features as needing no subscription, which sets it apart from Whoop. Some features in the Connect app are part of the optional Connect+ tier. Per Garmin's product listing, these include the **live in-workout view of current heart rate**, heart-rate zone displays and in-workout metric charts, along with calorie and nutrition guidance, Garmin Coach plans and some specialised workouts. Without Connect+, a user recording a workout sees their average heart rate for that workout, and the full data afterwards.

## How it was communicated

A Garmin press release and product page on 21 July 2026, with review units already with fitness-tech reviewers. Their hands-on coverage appeared the same day.
