# Outcome: Bungie restores vaulted Destiny 2 content

- 2026-09-21 — Bungie's new studio heads announce that vaulted campaigns, destinations and raids will return, with no dates, and that Marathon will be reworked ([Game Informer, 21 Sep 2026](https://gameinformer.com/2026/09/21/bungie-announces-plans-to-restore-vaulted-destiny-2-content-and-substantial-reworks-to)). - 2026-10 (exact date unverified) — God is a Geek reports Bungie saying vaulted campaigns will not return until early 2028 (search snippet only; not confirmed elsewhere; [God is a Geek](https://godisageek.com/2026/10/destiny-2-unvaulting-2028/)).

## Overall reaction

Positive. Players and creators who had campaigned against the end of Destiny 2 celebrated the news, and coverage described the reaction as cautiously positive. The doubts were about delivery, not the decision itself: Bungie's record of broken promises, the lack of any dates, whether a studio cut by layoffs can do the work, and whether the move is mainly a way to rescue the studio after Marathon underperformed. Nobody was reported as opposing the restoration.

## What people objected to

Caveats and concerns, not opposition:

- Trust: Bungie has made and broken similar commitments before.
- No timeline was given; a later report puts the campaigns' return in early 2028.
- A smaller studio, after layoffs, may lack the resources to rebuild the content.
- Restored content will make the game much bigger on disk.
- The move looks reactive, driven by Marathon's weak player numbers.

## Who pushed back

No group opposed it. Sceptics included long-time players wary of Bungie's record, some content creators and several commentators (The Outerhaven, FinalBoss).

## Did they back down?

No. The plan stood. The only later news was a reported timeline pushing the return of campaigns to early 2028, which is a schedule detail, not a change of decision, and is unconfirmed.
