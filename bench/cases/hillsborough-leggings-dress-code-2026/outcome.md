# Outcome: Hillsborough County leggings restriction

## Timeline

- 2026-07-14 — School board approves the 2026-27 code of conduct, including a rule that leggings and spandex must be covered to mid-thigh ([ABC Action News / Tampa Bay 28](https://www.tampabay28.com/news/local-news/hillsborough-county-school-board-reverses-decision-on-leggings-restrictions)).
- 2026-07-22 — A parent starts a Change.org petition asking the board to reverse the rule, arguing it targets girls, conflicts with district policy that dress rules apply equally to all, and was adopted without input from families ([Change.org petition](https://www.change.org/p/reverse-hillsborough-county-schools-dress-code-decision)).
- 2026-07-28 — Local press describes the code changes and the student-consultation process behind them ([Tampa Bay Beacons, 28 Jul 2026](https://www.tampabaybeacons.com/2026/07/28/hillsborough-changes-dress-code-after-talking-with-students/)).
- 2026-07-29 — Tampa Bay Times reports the district may ease the leggings rule after a parent outcry ([Tampa Bay Times](https://www.tampabay.com/news/hillsborough/2026/07/29/hillsborough-may-ease-student-leggings-restrictions-after-parent-outcry/)).
- 2026-08-03 — Bay News 9 reports the board will reconsider the rule; its story (updated 4 Aug) says the superintendent framed this as responding to family concerns ([Bay News 9, 3 Aug 2026](https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2026/08/03/hillsborough-school-board-reconsiders-dress-code-policy)).
- 2026-08-04 — Board votes 5-1 to drop the leggings and spandex restriction and return to the previous year's rules on that point; the pajama and slipper ban stays. The vice chair says her inbox was flooded with emails; the petition has passed 3,100 signatures. The board says it will revisit the dress code at a 29 September workshop ([FOX 13, 4 Aug 2026](https://www.fox13news.com/news/hillsborough-dress-code-school-board-reverses-ban-leggings.amp); [Bay News 9, 5 Aug 2026](https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2026/08/05/hillsborough-school-board-reverses-new-leggings-restrictions)).
- 2026-08-10 — School year starts under the previous year's rules on leggings ([FOX 13, 4 Aug 2026](https://www.fox13news.com/news/hillsborough-dress-code-school-board-reverses-ban-leggings.amp)).

## Overall reaction

Strongly negative, and fast. Within about a week of families learning of the rule, a petition was running and board members were getting hundreds of emails. Most objections were about the leggings line specifically; the other changes (skirt length, pajamas, spikes) drew little reported attention. The board reversed the leggings rule three weeks after adopting it, before school started.

## What people objected to

- The rule singled out girls, since leggings are mostly worn by female students, and sexualised ordinary clothing.
- It was unrealistic in Florida's heat and impractical for student athletes.
- It was adopted just before the school year without input from parents or students more broadly.
- It would make Hillsborough's code among the most restrictive of nearby districts.
- Parents said leggings had never caused a problem.

## Who pushed back

- Parents, led by the parent who started the Change.org petition (3,346 signatures by the time it was checked).
- Female students, including middle and high schoolers who spoke to the press and the board.
- Several board members, including the chair and vice chair, who moved to reverse it.

## Did they back down?

Yes, fully on the leggings rule. On 4 August 2026 the board voted 5-1 to restore the previous rule on leggings and spandex. One member voted no, saying the issue was spandex coverage in general, not girls. The rest of the new code (including the pajama ban) stayed. The board said the dress code could come back at a policy workshop set for 29 September.
