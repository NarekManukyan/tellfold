# Instagram refreshes its wordmark for the first time in about ten years

*Benchmark seed: a neutral summary of the announcement as it stood on 2026-08-13, written for the Tellfold accuracy test. Not the original text.*

## Who is announcing

Instagram is Meta's photo and video sharing app, used by well over two billion people. Its visual identity has been stable since 2016, when it introduced the pink-to-orange gradient camera icon and a cursive script wordmark, the "Instagram" text at the top of the app. Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, regularly announces product changes directly to users in short videos and posts on Instagram and Threads.

## What was announced

On Thursday 13 August 2026, Mosseri shared Instagram's first wordmark update in about a decade, along with a broader refreshed brand system.

- **New wordmark.** The "Instagram" text is redrawn as a straighter, tidier script. The loops are tighter and most letters are squared up, with the flourishes kept mainly on the "s", "r" and "g". It still references the original cursive.
- **App icon unchanged.** The gradient camera icon stays as it is.
- **Typefaces.** An updated Instagram Sans, plus two new families: Instagram Pen, a handwriting-style face, and Instagram Mono, a monospaced face.
- **Rollout.** The new wordmark starts rolling out globally at the top of the app immediately. Other parts of the brand system (motion, layouts and interface elements) will follow over time.
- **No functional change.** The update is visual. No new features, policies or prices come with it.

**Stated reasons:** Mosseri said the old wordmark had not changed in about ten years and it was time for a refresh. He described the new version as sharper and more modern, keeping a reference to the original and its simplicity. Instagram's director of product design said the team explored hundreds of directions and kept a script style because script reflects individuality. Meta's director of brand design said the system is meant to support the community's creativity and self-expression.

## How it was communicated

Mosseri posted the reveal on Instagram and Threads on 13 August 2026. Instagram and Meta design leads gave comments to design and tech press the same week.
