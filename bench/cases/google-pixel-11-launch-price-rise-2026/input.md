# Google launches the Pixel 11 range at $100 more per model, with 256 GB base storage

## Who the body is

Google makes the Pixel line of Android phones, watches and earbuds, and holds an annual "Made by Google" event each August to launch them. Pixels are known for their cameras and for showcasing Google's Gemini AI features. In 2026 a shortage of memory chips, driven by AI data centre demand, has pushed up component costs across the phone industry, and several makers have raised prices.

## What was announced

At Made by Google on Wednesday 12 August 2026, Google announced:

- **Pixel 11 ($899), Pixel 11 Pro ($1,099), Pixel 11 Pro XL ($1,299) and Pixel 11 Pro Fold ($1,899)**, each at least $100 more than the Pixel 10 equivalent, all running the new Tensor G6 chip.
- The 128 GB tier is dropped, so the Pixel 11 and 11 Pro now start at 256 GB, double last year's base storage.
- A redesigned camera bar that Google says is over 40% thinner, new colours, brighter screens and tougher, more scratch-resistant glass on the Pro models.
- "HiLight", a light in the camera flash area of the Pro models that shows caller-specific colours and Gemini's status.
- A lighter, thinner Pro Fold with a redesigned hinge that Google says is three times more durable.
- New AI features: Gemini acting across apps, "Rambler" voice dictation that handles filler words, Circle to Search from the camera, and Live Transcribe for American Sign Language.
- **Pixel Tag**, a $29 Bluetooth tracker ($99 for four) using Android's Find Hub network, arriving in November.
- **Pixel Watch 5** from $399, visually similar to the Pixel Watch 4, plus a Stephen Curry edition; a new colour for Pixel Buds Pro 2.

## Stated reasons

- Google presented the phones as the best showcase for Gemini, with AI that helps across apps and the camera.
- The higher prices come with double the base storage; industry-wide memory costs form the backdrop.

## How it was communicated

A live-streamed launch event, Google's product blog posts and press briefings, with pre-orders opening the same day.
