# McLaren unveils a new wordmark and brand identity for its road cars and racing teams

## Who the body is

McLaren is a British maker of high-performance sports cars (McLaren Automotive) and a motor racing organisation (McLaren Racing) that runs teams in Formula 1 and IndyCar and is preparing a World Endurance Championship entry. It was founded by New Zealand driver Bruce McLaren. Its F1 team, in papaya orange since 2017, won both F1 world titles the previous season, with Lando Norris as drivers' champion. McLaren Automotive is investing about £500 million in manufacturing and engineering in England, plans its first SUV and a new hybrid supercar, and in 2026 has been telling heritage-themed stories, including a retro-styled grand tourer. Its existing logo pairs a "McLaren" wordmark with the "Speedmark" swoosh.

## What was announced

On 22 September 2026, shortly before the Azerbaijan Grand Prix, McLaren revealed a refreshed brand identity for both the automotive and racing businesses:

- A new "McLaren" wordmark based on the sign above the service station in Remuera, Auckland, owned by Bruce McLaren's parents, where he built his first race-winning car. It uses a lighter sans-serif and a smaller, underlined "c".
- A dedicated typeface, McLaren Sans.
- The Speedmark stays and papaya stays the core colour, with an expanded palette (amber, midnight, black, anthracite, silver, white) drawn from moments in the company's history. A "Speedy Kiwi" emblem referencing Bruce McLaren's roots is kept.
- The identity will be rolled out in stages across vehicles, apparel and digital content.

## Stated reasons

- To bring the racing and road-car brands together in a "clearer and more unified expression" of McLaren.
- To reconnect the company with its founder and origins while signalling its future ambitions, described by executives as the start of a new chapter.
- To celebrate symbols that fans, partners and staff already know.

## How it was communicated

A company announcement, a post from the F1 team on X, and statements from McLaren Automotive's chief creative officer and McLaren Racing's chief marketing officer.
