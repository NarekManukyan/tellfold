# Outcome: HubSpot Contact Discovery data-sharing terms (announced 2026-07-01)

## Timeline

- 2026-07-01 — HubSpot publishes updated terms and announces Contact Discovery for 4 August, with enrichment data shared across customers by default ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/hubspot-kills-contact-discovery-terms-after-customer-backlash/); [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/hubspot-reverses-customer-data-enrichment-plan-after-customer-backlash/)).
- 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-05 — Criticism spreads, mostly on LinkedIn among sales leaders and RevOps teams. Fractional CRO Saarika Chotai shares the email and calls it "another reason to boycott" HubSpot. Others say they will switch CRM ([Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/hubspot-backtracks-after-ai-data-sharing-controversy/); [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/hubspot-reverses-customer-data-ai-feature-opt-out)).
- ~2026-07-05 — A HubSpot Community thread asks how the AI-training and enrichment settings interact and whether opt-outs apply backwards ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/hubspot-kills-contact-discovery-terms-after-customer-backlash/)).
- 2026-07-05 — CPTO Duncan Lennox posts "We got this wrong, and we are fixing it": HubSpot will not go ahead with the 1 July changes, and any future use will be fully opt-in. Co-founder Dharmesh Shah replies on LinkedIn: "We made a mistake and are reversing that decision." ([CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/hubspot-reverses-customer-data-enrichment-plan-after-customer-backlash/); [Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/hubspot-backtracks-after-ai-data-sharing-controversy/)).
- 2026-07-06 — Lennox shares the retraction on LinkedIn. Chief Customer Officer John Dick edits his launch post to say "You control your data." ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/hubspot-kills-contact-discovery-terms-after-customer-backlash/); [Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/hubspot-backtracks-after-ai-data-sharing-controversy/)).
- 2026-07-08 — TNW summarises the episode: "The revolt took four days" ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/hubspot-reverses-customer-data-ai-feature-opt-out)).

## Overall reaction

Negative among HubSpot customers, especially B2B sales and revenue-operations professionals, who saw it as HubSpot taking data they had built up and sharing it with other customers, including competitors, without asking. A minority (e.g. Growthly founder Fruzsina Peti) saw real value in a shared dataset and blamed poor communication rather than the idea.

## What people objected to

- Opt-out by default: customers were enrolled automatically instead of being asked ([CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/hubspot-reverses-customer-data-enrichment-plan-after-customer-backlash/)).
- The CRM data customers built belongs to them, not to HubSpot to redistribute ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/hubspot-reverses-customer-data-ai-feature-opt-out); [Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/hubspot-backtracks-after-ai-data-sharing-controversy/)).
- Their contacts' details could reach other businesses, possibly competitors ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/hubspot-kills-contact-discovery-terms-after-customer-backlash/)).
- If HubSpot wants customers' contact data, it should pay for it; some called the move desperate ([CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/hubspot-reverses-customer-data-enrichment-plan-after-customer-backlash/)).
- Confusing controls: it was unclear how the enrichment and AI-training opt-outs interacted, and whether they applied backwards ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/hubspot-kills-contact-discovery-terms-after-customer-backlash/)).

## Who pushed back

B2B sales leaders, fractional CROs and RevOps practitioners on LinkedIn, HubSpot Community members and admins, and some threatening to switch CRM. The trade press (CMSWire, Salesforce Ben, PPC Land, TNW) amplified it.

## Did they back down?

Yes, fully, within about four days. HubSpot dropped the 1 July terms changes, apologised publicly at C-level, and promised any future version will be fully opt-in. It did not say whether Contact Discovery will return in another form.
