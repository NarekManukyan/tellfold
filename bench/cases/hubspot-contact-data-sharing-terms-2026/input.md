# HubSpot updates its customer terms ahead of Contact Discovery

*Benchmark seed: a neutral summary of the announcement as it stood on 2026-07-01, written for the Tellfold accuracy test. Not the original text.*

## Who is announcing

HubSpot is a customer relationship management (CRM) and marketing software company. Its customers are mostly small and mid-sized businesses, plus marketing and sales agencies. They use it to store contacts, run email campaigns, manage sales pipelines and handle customer service. HubSpot offers an optional **data enrichment** feature that fills in missing details on contact and company records, such as job titles or company information. It also sells AI tools under the Breeze brand.

## What was announced

On 1 July 2026 HubSpot emailed customers and published updates, effective that day, to its Customer Terms of Service, Product Specific Terms, Privacy Policy, Data Processing Agreement and list of sub-processors. The update prepares for a new prospecting feature, **Contact Discovery**, due to launch on **4 August 2026**.

- From 4 August, for accounts with data enrichment switched on, certain enrichment data may be **shared with other HubSpot customers** as part of a shared commercial dataset that keeps improving over time.
- **In scope:** "business card-level" details (name, job title, company, work email, employer) and email deliverability signals.
- **Out of scope:** email content, notes, deals, call recordings and custom fields. HubSpot says it does not read or share email content.
- **Default:** Accounts with enrichment on take part automatically. To opt out, customers can turn off data enrichment (Settings > Data Management > Data Enrichment) or opt out of AI model training through a separate setting.
- **Other changes in the same update:** the "Machine Learning and AI" section is renamed "Artificial Intelligence" and gets a new "Agentic AI" subsection, Commerce Hub is renamed Revenue Hub, three AI and data sub-processors are added (Baseten Labs, Bright Data, Exa Labs), and new privacy-policy disclosures cover email engagement data, reCAPTCHA and Google Maps.

**Stated reasons:** HubSpot says the change will help customers prospect in a more trusted, effective way. Its framing is "Trusted Prospecting": a shared, continuously refined dataset gives more robust and reliable contact data and better deliverability, moving outbound sales away from generic bought lists and mass campaigns. The company stresses that only professional, business-card-level information is involved.

## How it was communicated

An email to customers and updated legal pages on 1 July 2026, a post in the HubSpot Community, and LinkedIn posts by HubSpot executives announcing Contact Discovery's 4 August launch.
