# Outcome: United MileagePlus spending-only status for 2027

## Timeline

- 2026-10-13 — United announces the end of flight-based qualification and a higher 1K threshold. One Mile at a Time calls the loss of flexibility less than ideal and says 1K will be materially harder to earn; a Silver member in the comments says he will probably lose status and may switch airlines ([One Mile at a Time, 13 Oct 2026](https://onemileatatime.com/news/united-mileageplus-elite-status-harder-earn-winning-overly-confident/)).
- 2026-10-13 — Live and Let's Fly argues United risks losing customers who fall just short; most substantive reader comments are critical (a 1K of over a decade says he "will be searching", another says programmes treat members "like fools"), while a few accept it as a spending push or an offset for higher fares ([Live and Let's Fly, 13 Oct 2026](https://liveandletsfly.com/united-mileageplus-status-changes-2027/)).
- 2026-10-15 — InsideFlyer, summarising another blog, warns that chasing status can mean costly detours and card spending diverted from better rewards ([InsideFlyer, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/united-ends-flight-based-status-qualification-in-2027/)).
- 2026-10-19 — AFAR quotes NerdWallet's Sally French (it "removes confusion", but members who flew enough without the spend could be upset) and point.me's Jordan Rozum (United is "doubling down" on spend; tier-chasing likely no longer makes sense for newcomers) ([AFAR, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.afar.com/magazine/united-drops-flight-based-status-new-mileageplus-math)).
- 2026-10-22 — Delta confirms its own thresholds will not change; coverage contrasts this with United's tougher rules ([The Points Guy, 22 Oct 2026](https://thepointsguy.com/news/delta-medallion-2027-requirements/)).

The rules stand as announced ([TheTravel, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.thetravel.com/united-airlines-plans-more-changes-after-stripping-mileage-perk-from-passengers-without-150-credit-card/); [TheTravel, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.thetravel.com/united-is-ending-flight-based-elite-status-what-mileageplus-flyers-gain-and-lose/)).

## Overall reaction

Negative among frequent flyers and the loyalty press. The main complaint is that frequent, lower-fare flyers lose their route to status while the 1K bar rises. A minority of commenters and analysts accepted it as simpler or as aimed at high spenders, and premium travellers on expense accounts were less affected.

## What people objected to

- Removing the flight-count path hurts frequent flyers who buy cheaper fares, such as regional commuters and road warriors.
- The 1K jump to 32,000 PQPs (about $32,000 of airfare) is a big premium over rivals.
- United asks for more while giving less loyalty in return; members may take their business elsewhere.
- More weight on co-branded credit card spending, including tying the starter deposit to a United card.
- Demand-based PlusPoints upgrade pricing adds uncertainty.

## Who pushed back

- High-segment, lower-spend frequent flyers and long-time 1K members.
- Loyalty bloggers (One Mile at a Time, Live and Let's Fly).
- Some members threatening to move to Delta, Alaska or others.

## Did they back down?

No. As of 5 November 2026 the rules were reported as taking effect on 1 January 2027 unchanged.
