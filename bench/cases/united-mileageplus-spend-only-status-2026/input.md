# United makes MileagePlus elite status spending-only from 2027 and raises the Premier 1K bar

## Who the body is

United Airlines is one of the three largest US airlines. Its MileagePlus frequent flyer programme has four Premier elite tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum and 1K) that give perks such as upgrades, preferred seats, priority boarding and free bags. Today members can qualify in two ways: on spending alone, measured in Premier qualifying points (PQPs, earned mostly from airfare and partly from United co-branded credit card spending), or on a mix of flights (Premier qualifying flights, PQFs) and fewer PQPs. Earlier in 2026 United cut the miles earned on standard fares by members without a United credit card from 5 to 3 per dollar, and it had already said that upgrades paid for with PlusPoints would move to demand-based pricing in 2027.

## What was announced

On Tuesday 13 October 2026 United set out its Premier qualification rules for activity in calendar 2027 (which decides status for 2028):

- The flight-based route is removed. Status will be based on PQPs only.
- Thresholds: Silver 6,000 PQPs, Gold 12,000, Platinum 18,000 (the same as today's points-only route) and 1K 32,000, up from 28,000.
- The mixed routes that disappear were 15, 30, 45 or 60 flights combined with 5,000, 10,000, 15,000 or 22,000 PQPs.
- Every tier still requires at least four flights on United or United Express.
- The annual starter PQP deposit (about 5% of each threshold) will go only to members holding an eligible United credit card by 31 December 2026.
- From 1 February 2027 the PlusPoints needed for upgrades by Platinum and 1K members will vary with demand.
- Core Premier benefits (upgrades, seating, priority services, bags) are unchanged, and existing status carries over.

## Stated reasons

- A United spokesperson said the changes make earning status simpler and easier to track.
- United said the rules should more directly reflect how customers engage with and invest in MileagePlus.

## How it was communicated

A programme update to members and the MileagePlus website, with a spokesperson statement to travel media.
