# Delta keeps its Medallion elite status spending thresholds unchanged for 2027

## Who the body is

Delta Air Lines is one of the three largest US airlines. Its SkyMiles programme has four public Medallion elite tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond) that bring perks such as upgrades, preferred seats and priority services. Since a 2023 overhaul, Medallion status has been earned only through Medallion Qualifying Dollars (MQDs): one MQD per dollar spent on Delta-marketed flights and Delta Vacations, a share of partner flight spending, and MQDs from spending on certain Delta American Express cards, some of which also give a 2,500 MQD head start each year. Delta lowered the thresholds in its original 2023 proposal by about 15% before they took effect and has not changed them since. Nine days earlier, United Airlines announced it would drop flight counts from its status rules for 2027 and raise its top tier from 28,000 to 32,000 qualifying points, and Southwest has also overhauled its programme for 2027.

## What was announced

On Thursday 22 October 2026 a Delta spokesperson confirmed that Medallion thresholds will stay the same for status earned through activity in 2027, the third year in a row without an increase:

- Silver: 5,000 MQDs
- Gold: 10,000 MQDs
- Platinum: 15,000 MQDs
- Diamond: 28,000 MQDs

Earning rules are unchanged, including the credit card routes. MQDs reset to zero on 1 January 2027 and do not roll over; status earned in 2027 lasts for the rest of 2027 and all of 2028.

## Stated reasons

Delta did not give a detailed reason beyond confirming the thresholds. It had previously said it wanted to avoid frequent changes to the programme after 2023.

## How it was communicated

A spokesperson's confirmation to travel media, reflected on Delta's Medallion status pages.
