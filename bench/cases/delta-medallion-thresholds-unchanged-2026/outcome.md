# Outcome: Delta keeps Medallion thresholds unchanged

## Timeline

- 2026-10-22 — The Points Guy reports Delta's confirmation and calls it "certainly welcome" news for members, contrasting it with United's tougher rules ([The Points Guy, 22 Oct 2026](https://thepointsguy.com/news/delta-medallion-2027-requirements/)).
- 2026-10-22 — Upgraded Points calls the decision "welcome predictability" for Delta flyers after years of programme changes ([Upgraded Points, updated 22 Oct 2026](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/delta-skymiles-2027-medallion-status/)). The Points Party reports it neutrally as planning information ([The Points Party, 22 Oct 2026](https://thepointsparty.com/articles/delta-keeps-medallion-requirements-same-2027)).
- 2026-10-23 — Thrifty Traveler calls it "legitimately good" news and a win for frequent flyers, while stressing that the bar is still "a *lot* of money", that it only means things are not getting worse, and that status matters to a tiny share of travellers. It says some staff had expected an increase ([Thrifty Traveler, 23 Oct 2026](https://thriftytraveler.com/?p=147883)). InsideFlyer relays the news, noting some observers had expected higher spending requirements ([InsideFlyer, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/delta-keeps-medallion-status-thresholds-unchanged-for-2027/)).
- 2026-11-06 — InsideFlyer, relaying TPG, adds cautions: some cheap fares earn no MQDs, and reaching high tiers through card spending is a big financial commitment ([InsideFlyer, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/delta-keeps-2027-medallion-qualification-requirements-unchanged/)).

## Overall reaction

Positive, with caveats. Loyalty media treated it as good news, especially next to United's and Southwest's tougher 2027 rules. No criticism of the decision itself was found; the caveats were that thresholds set in 2023 remain high and that status chasing is costly.

## What people objected to

Little objection. Caveats raised:

- The thresholds are still a lot of money; "not worse" is not the same as good.
- Status matters only to a small minority of travellers, and many are better off without chasing it.
- Qualification depends on spending (including big card spend), and some discounted fares earn no MQDs.

## Who pushed back

No group pushed back in the sources found. Loyalty commentators (The Points Guy, Thrifty Traveler, Upgraded Points) welcomed it.

## Did they back down?

No, and there was no pressure to; the thresholds stand.
