# Quake Champions drops free-to-play, becomes a paid Steam game and ends content updates

## Who the body is

Quake Champions is an arena shooter from id Software, published by Bethesda; both are part of Microsoft's Xbox division. It came out in 2017 and has been free-to-play for years, earning money from a store, loot boxes, a battle pass and two virtual currencies (Platinum, bought with real money, and Shards, earned in play). It has been sold on Steam and the Microsoft Store. Its player base is small: SteamDB showed a 24-hour peak of about 600 players around the time of the change.

## What was announced

On Wednesday 23 September 2026 id Software released update 1.31 (the "Point Release") with an FAQ and a Steam announcement:

- **Paid ownership:** Quake Champions is no longer free. It is now a paid game sold only on Steam (£8.99 in the UK) and has been removed from the Microsoft Store.
- **Existing players keep it:** anyone who played on Steam before 23 September is upgraded to the full game at no cost. Microsoft Store players can link their Bethesda.net and Steam accounts to transfer access; the simple linking route runs until 7 October, after which requests go to customer support case by case. Rankings, profiles and progress carry over.
- **Monetisation removed:** Platinum and Shards, the battle pass, the store, loot boxes, daily login rewards and weekly and event challenges are gone. All champions and most cosmetics are unlocked for everyone. Platinum bought in the previous 60 days is refunded.
- **Game changes:** a reworked progression system up to level 999, a vote system to remove abusive players or cheaters, starter weapons, interface and performance options, and fewer data centre locations to concentrate players and speed up matchmaking.
- **Future:** "No additional content updates are planned at this time."

## Stated reasons

The FAQ described the move as a shift to a paid ownership model. No wider rationale was given in the announcement material reported.

## How it was communicated

The 1.31 patch, an official FAQ and a Steam news post on 23 September, with no advance notice.
