# Outcome: Quake Champions becomes a paid game

## Timeline

- 2026-09-23 — id Software releases update 1.31 and moves Quake Champions to a paid Steam-only model, unlocking all champions and cosmetics and removing currencies. Ei Nerd calls the change surprising and a "quiet victory" for the arena-shooter community, while noting the steep learning curve may make new buyers hard to find ([Ei Nerd, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.einerd.com/quake-champions-pago-atualizacao-1-31-fim-microtransacoes/)).
- 2026-09-24 — GamingOnLinux calls it "quite an odd move" for a game with a 24-hour peak of 596 players and asks who will pay now. Its readers split: some welcome it ("Maybe I'll finally play it now"), others object to paying for an abandoned game ("Pay for a game we've abandoned") or expect the servers to close eventually ([GamingOnLinux, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/09/quake-champions-goes-from-free-to-paid-with-no-more-content-updates-planned/)). LevelUpTalk reports a "wave of discontent" among players worried about the game's future and that a price will deter new players (search snippet only; [LevelUpTalk](https://leveluptalk.com/news/quake-champions-paid-update-steam-transition/)). Gamereactor and Power Up Gaming report the details without comment ([Gamereactor, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.gamereactor.eu/quake-champions-paid-model/); [Power Up Gaming, 24 Sep 2026](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/09/24/quake-champions-to-end-free-to-play-and-remove-in-game-currencies/)).

## Overall reaction

Mixed, and quiet. Existing players got everything unlocked for free and lost the monetisation they disliked, which some welcomed. But the change was read by many as the end of active support: charging new players for a game with no planned updates and a tiny population struck commentators as odd and worried players about the game's future. Coverage was limited to gaming sites; there was no large organised response.

## What people objected to

- Paying for a game that will get no more content updates.
- A price barrier will deter the new players a small community needs.
- Fears that official servers will eventually be shut down.
- Missing features the community wants if support is ending, such as mod support and a server browser.

## Who pushed back

- Existing and lapsed Quake Champions players worried about the game's future (GamingOnLinux readers, LevelUpTalk).
- Commentators questioning whether anyone will pay for it (GamingOnLinux).

Some players, and Ei Nerd's writer, welcomed the removal of microtransactions and the free unlocks.

## Did they back down?

No. No change to the paid model was reported in the sources checked.
